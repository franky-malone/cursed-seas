A mighty gladiator goliath woman that bested Golt in a 1vs1 fight on the
Royal Road direction to Orchiva.

She used a spear and a shield as her weapons and had unending stamina.

After the fight, and still wounded she run towards Puerto Ballena's "El
Barrizal" claming that the strength of her ancestors was running through
her.
