Dienstag, 17. Februar 2026


- The hill giants have a tattoo of a red fist on an arm: symbol of the
  Red Fangs tribe that attacked Kespien's home

    giants in their group at that point

    we fight
  - Kespien lands Belmont's Blitz twice, Jori summons wolves, CV
    hypnotizes, Golt smites and we manage to kill the giants
- The giants have a huge sack: wagon wheel, pig, wool pot, grain, beets,
  chest
  - The chest was trapped but one of the masons opens it for us
  - Contents: 32 GP, 118 SP, 213 CP, silver brooch shaped like a tulip
    (25 GP), 1 healing potion, 1 potion of animal friendship, a notebook
    with delivery routes from Puerto Ballena to Nersand with the family
    name "von Kohlendorf" appearing several times
- We report back to the masons who are very thankful
  - Caravans will be able to cross the bridge in 3 weeks
  - CV informs Jhandril and asks for reinforcements
- The masons help us cross the broken bridge
- CV observes green lights on the road in the south, similar to lights
  that come from bones in a cemetary
  - CV sees lights approaching on the road: large mausoleum pulled by
    two ogres
  - Spooky!
