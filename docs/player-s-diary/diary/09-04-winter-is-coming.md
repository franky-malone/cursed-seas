Sonntag, 6. Juli 2025


- - After leaving the hut we fight against the winter wolves:
    - Virgula shows that he is also capable of dagger throwing
    - Kespien sleeps the last wolf that tried to run away, which is then
      finished off by Golt
    - We take their pelts and fangs
  - We meet up again with Thrag after calling him with the horn:
    - Thrag comes with a large herd of bisons, which seem charmed
    - He leads us to his house, a small hut with the walls decorated
      with skulls of predator animals
    - Most of the grass in the valley is eaten, not enough to sustain
      large herd
    - Thrag brings the wolf cub to fill the vase with ist breath
    - He provides us with warm winter clothes and the amulet made of
      bison's
      horn
    - Jori has a vision of his spirit totems
    - Advice for wyvern hunt: go into the mountains before winter, there
      will be snowstorms within a week
    - The white wyvern decimated the wyvern population
  - Travel for 2 days into the mountains:
    - We encounter a young wyvern that has parts of its tail missing
    - A giant white wyvern scoops down from the sky to attack the
      smaller one and rip out ist stinger
