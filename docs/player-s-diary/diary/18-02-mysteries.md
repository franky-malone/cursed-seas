Montag, 2. März 2026


- - Spooky caravan approaches:
    - 3 knights with radiant lions uniform, look lifeless and do not
      respond (all guards are undead)
    - 4 hobgoblins flank us
    - A creepy black dragonborn exits the caravan, and shows interest in
      Marsander in conversation with CV
    - We play CV's servants (not very convincing)
    - He goes and wakes the master, an identical dragonborn with a large
      staff, who introduces himself as Schwefel Sulphure
    - Asks about the Onyx Bishop, a tower that originally was in
      Marsander
    - The tower survived the destruction but was moved to the Dunkleloch
    - Schwefel Sulphure ask for CV's memories about Marsander and offer
      a resurrection spell in exchange
    - CV chooses to not resurrect a Marsandian from his past, because we
      hope his brother is still alive and his parents died peacefully
      before the destruction
    - CV accepts the magical staff instead and Sulphure probes into his
      mind
      - CV charmed a noble to receive the count title
      - New memories unlocked: CV was abducted and remembers queen
        Sanderia from prison "these are our new test subjects"
      - CV remembers the Onyx Tower next to the palace of the queen,
        after the catastrophe it vanished
      - Shadow shaped like a bat surrounds CV, detaches and flies away
      - CV feels empty, like something is missing
      - Sulfure looks disappointed but gives CV the "Staff of Sulphurous
        Whispers"
    - Kespien sends a warning about Sulphure to Jhandril and urges him
      to inform the radiant lions
  - Long rest on the road
  - We meet a caravan on the road:
    - Beware of Orchiva, but not affected after 1 night
    - "Von Kohlendorf"=important noble woman from Puerto Ballena
    - They sell wool bracelets again the pest (but the group tells Jori
      he should not buy it)
  - We hear a grunting from behind: tall female goliath named Vailea
    Stoneblood, breaker of peaks
    - She challenges Golt to a duel to test his strength, she wins in a
      close fight
    - She says her next challenge is the El Barrizal in Puerto Ballena
  - Long rest
    - Jori is visited by his spirits, who voice concerns that the
      connection to nature/spirits is disturbed near Orchiva, they count
      on him to restore balance
  - Number of insects increase while approaching Orchiva, less rabbits
    etc
  - We arrive in Orchiva
    - Houses have rings of wool on doors
    - Tavern "Ringed Octopus" lead by old tiefling Lucien
    - Since several months: crops are rotting, insects appearing
    - Golt talks to Roy, whose daughter Rosaline was the first one taken
      by the plague
    - Rosaline was found outside and brought to Unvera in the middle of
      the night
    - We visit Sister Unvera (halfling), who repurposed an animal clinic
      into a hospice
    - Patients are itching, scratching themselves, crawling sensation,
      respiratory problems, bite marks from the inside, high fever,
      chills, not spreading through contact
    - Jori senses that the disease is not a poison, it is non-natural,
      magical
    - Inus ("the pale one"): order of the white scale (Kelemvor), rented
      cottage outside of town
