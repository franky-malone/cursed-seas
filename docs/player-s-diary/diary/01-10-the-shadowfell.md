  - Ralto's quest for us: Bring back Urs!
    - lived on the material plane hundred of years ago, talented smith,
      good friend
    - Ralto teleported them back but got separated from Urs
    - Infected with Ursanthropy (big bear man), curse is a special one
      (especially bad), Urs might have lost his mind
    - Ralto found that Urs is stranded in the Shadowfell
    - Ralto purchased magical bracelets from the Amirah
    - Ralto will call us once he has everything prepared (but can wait)
      and open a portal to the Shadowfell and back for us
    - If we bring back Urs with a controlled curse, Ralto can open a
      portal to the Amirah's home for us
    - Corsina gives us a recipe for a venom, mix these ingredients:
      - The pistil (violet) of a nightshade (ashen)
      - The web of a phase spider: phase between ethereal plane and
        Shadowfell, harvest from dead one
      - The seeds of the midnight bloom: blue-violet flower that grows
        in wet environment
      - Bring any spare ingredients we can find to Corsina
    - Tips:
      - Shadowfell is a distorted dark mirror of the material plane
      - It is very dark, don't get lost!
      - Do not eat anything from the Shadowfell
      - Creatures from the Shadowfell are attracted by light
      - The use of magic is impacted: the weave is unstable, cast a
        leveled spell -/> spellcasting ability check, spell might be
        weakened or cancelled
      - Specific types of spells that don't do well in Shadowfell: fire,
        radiant, magical light
      - Spells that do well: necrotic, creation of darkness, spells
        related to shadows
      - The landscape can change, difficult to navigate
