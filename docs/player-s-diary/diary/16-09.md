- Urs appears as a huge brown bear surrounded by shadow tendrils
  - Golt attacks Urs, Urs charges him
  - Golt convinces Urs to let us go
  - Urs chews something from his bag, human Urs seems to come through and tells us to run
  - We run!
- We travel through the area and see shadows moving in the water
- We find a clearing in the forest with a bed of flowers
  - Flowers are pulsing rhythmically
  - Kespien casts Light on a coin, we see violet flower pistils, silver pollen and red soil
  - Kespien inhales pollen and hallucinates of being in a different place: patrolling soldiers with spiky ears and white skim like CV
  - Golt brings him back with a slap
  - Jori hallucinates of a market place of pale white-haired people looking like Dash, a bit smaller than humans
  - We manage to harvest 1 dose of nightshade pistil
  - All flowers close and whither when taken
- Creatures (meenlocks) manifest from the shadows and surprise us!
  - CV and Jori are paralyzed
  - Kespien and Golt attack!
  - CV and Golt hallucinate of a place resembling Marsander before the catastrophe: market, scene changes to marching soldiers, people fleeing, fire spreading, ruins, massive black hole in the sky
  - Kespien steals CV's stone
  - Kespien draws a mustache on Golt
- Kespien's summoned hut drops during the night
- Kespien attunes to Schwefel Sulphure's staff: strong sulfur odor, disadvantage on CHA checks except advantage on Intimidation checks
- We track spiders to the east
  - Something is watching and whispering
  - We see ruins and approach
  - While we investigate, shadows take form: "you did it", "we suffer for her", "we want revenge"
  - We get surrounded by a flesh creature and three undead
