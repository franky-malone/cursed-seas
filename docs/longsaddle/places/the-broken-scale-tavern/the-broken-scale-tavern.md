## Overview

**The Broken Scale Tavern** is the most popular tavern and inn in [Longsaddle](/longsaddle/).

It is owned by [Vorskann](./vorskann), a copper dragonborn whose loud and cheerful personality has become almost as famous as the tavern itself.

There are usually two signs that tell travellers they are getting close to the Broken Scale: **music pouring out through its doors or the booming sound of Vorskann's laughter.**

## 🍰 Special Food

### Dragon's Eye Dessert

A decadent dessert resembling the eye of a dragon: a sphere of **chocolate ganache encased in edible gold leaf**, served atop a bed of raspberry coulis.

---

## 💨 Other Amenities

### Dragon's Schicha

A special shisha that allows those smoking it to **create figures in the air with the smoke**.

### Live Music

Vorskann loves live music, and it is rare for a night to pass without someone performing at the Broken Scale.

Many of the performers are **bards visiting Longsaddle to join the Temple**, who play at the tavern in exchange for food or accommodation.
