## Scroll Prices

| Spell Level | Price | Availability |
|---|---:|---|
| Cantrip | 30 gp | Always available |
| 1st | 60 gp | Always available |
| 2nd | 120 gp | 80% chance of having the desired spell |
| 3rd | 300 gp | 50% chance of having the desired spell |
| 4th | 750 gp | 30% chance of having the desired spell |
| 5th | 1,500 gp | 10% chance of having the desired spell |

> **Material Components:** If a spell requires consumable material components, the price of the scroll may be increased accordingly.
