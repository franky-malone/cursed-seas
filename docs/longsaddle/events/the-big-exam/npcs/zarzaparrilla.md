## Overview

**Zarzaparrilla** is an unusual firbolg from the **Crispy Woods** and a devout member of the **Circle of the Green Antlers**.

Towering over most other applicants, with moss and twigs tangled in her hair, she cuts an imposing figure. Yet when she speaks, it is usually in sharp bursts of only two or three words, delivered in a shrill voice more reminiscent of a startled forest bird than a creature of her stature.

## Voice of the Woods

Zarzaparrilla claims to speak for **the roots, the sap, and the wind**.

Her loyalty lies with the spirits of the woods and the whispering groves—and, occasionally, the moon. She pays far more attention to the voices of trees and soil than to those of scholars or teachers.

Her connection to nature is strange even by firbolg standards, but her knowledge of the natural world runs remarkably deep.

## Personality

While firbolgs are often known for their quiet wisdom, Zarzaparrilla possesses something considerably wilder.

There is an unsettling curiosity behind her pale eyes, and she has a habit of silently staring at others before suddenly offering cryptic observations such as:

> **"Too many questions."**

> **"Tree remembers everything."**

Her strange behavior often leaves other students unsure whether she is profoundly insightful, completely unpredictable, or perhaps a little of both.

---

## The Temple of Knowledge

Zarzaparrilla did not come to the [Temple of Knowledge](/factions/temple-of-knowledge/) because of its books or scholarly reputation.

She came because **something in the soil told her to**.

Whatever message she believes brought her to the Temple, Zarzaparrilla has taken her place among the other applicants seriously.

She may listen more closely to bark than to teachers, but make no mistake:

**She is here to win.**
