Thursday, April 25, 2024

1:12 PM

A member of the "Order of the White Scale", located in the the Scale
Hills to the north, passed towards Orchiva a tenday ago. He was pale as
ivory, with large circles under his eyes and ashen hair and said that
Kelemvor had guided him in that direction.



There has been sightings of a big white flying creature to the east. It
is unsure of what it is, but for the size of it, it easily could be a
white dragon.



Some locals have been trying a new drug called Espuma de Mar that comes
from Puerto Ballena. Rumors has it, it can improve your spell casting
skills.



There have been bandits assaulting drink caravans coming from Kor
Kuldir. If the next caravan doesn't come soon, the reserves of alcohol
supplies could come short.
