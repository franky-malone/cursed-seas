An awakened gorilla named Koko Bananicus, the Sacred Plume of
Numeromancy. He stands upright with a calm, calculating expression and
wise, intelligent eyes. His dark fur is partially covered by intricate
robes embroidered with glowing arcane symbols, mathematical formulas,
and constellations. A monocle with etched runes rests over one eye. He
carries a staff topped with rotating number-inscribed rings and dangling
abacus beads. Around his neck hangs a large pendant shaped like a
stylized infinity symbol. Scrolls, wax tablets, and old tomes peek from
a leather satchel slung over one shoulder.
