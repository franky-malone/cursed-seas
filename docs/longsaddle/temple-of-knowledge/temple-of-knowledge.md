Description



In the heart of Longsaddle, nestled within the embrace of the city's
bustling streets and winding alleys, lies the revered sanctum known as
the Temple of Knowledge. A bastion of erudition and enlightenment, this
grand edifice stands as a beacon for scholars, arcanists, bards, and all
seekers of wisdom who dare to tread its hallowed halls. Whispers of old
claim that within its labyrinthine depths resides the sum total of
mortal understanding, preserved within the countless tomes and ancient
scrolls that line its shelves. A common saying in Norberia tells “If
it ain't in the Temple's books, it ain't nowhere yet"



Temple areas



The temple is divided in three major sections:



The Oratorium --/>  With its open-air design and sweeping arches, the
Oratorium welcomes all who seek enlightenment, offering a space where
minds can gather, ideas can flourish, and the pursuit of wisdom knows no
bounds. The Oratorium offers a platform for individuals to share their
expertise and insights through talks and presentations. Prospective
speakers must submit applications to the Temple for licensure, which,
upon approval, grants them the privilege to conduct their discourse
within the Oratorium. Speakers are expected to uphold some simple
guidelines set by the Temple, ensuring a respectful and inclusive
environment conducive to intellectual discourse. Talks held within the
Oratorium are open to all who wish to attend, fostering a community of
learning and exchange.



The Hall of lore --/> These majestic halls are accessible to all who
seek enlightenment. Visitors are granted the opportunity to engage with
the temple's teachings and delve into its repository of knowledge,
albeit with a modest fee to ensure the maintenance of the location.
Within its halls, esteemed members of the temple lead classes and
colloquiums, sharing their expertise with those willing to invest in
their pursuit of wisdom. Meanwhile, the meticulously curated collection
of books and manuscripts awaits, offering a gateway to the boundless
realms of learning contained within the Hall of Lore's storied shelves.



The Sanctum of Eternal Wisdom --/> The Sanctum of Eternal Wisdom stands
as the inner sanctum of the Temple of Knowledge, accessible exclusively
to its esteemed members. Within its sacred confines lies the very
essence of enlightenment, where the vast majority of the temple's
knowledge is preserved and disseminated. Here, higher-ranking members
conduct regular classes, imparting their profound insights and guiding
disciples along the path of understanding. Study rooms, ensconced in a
permanent silence effect to foster uninterrupted contemplation, offer
sanctuaries for members to delve into the depths of their chosen
subjects. Moreover, the sanctum boasts a meticulously organized library,
sorted alphabetically by every conceivable topic known to mortal minds,
ensuring that seekers of wisdom may access the accumulated knowledge of
ages past with ease and precision.





How is the temple organized?



Within the Sanctum of Eternal Wisdom, their halls are filled with
diminutive automatons known as Modrons, whispered to be the ancient
architects of the temple itself, tasked with the sacred duty of
gathering and safeguarding the repository of knowledge within its halls.
The mysteries surrounding these enigmatic constructs abound, as none can
fathom the precise nature of their origins or the purpose behind their
meticulous labors. Yet, their unwavering dedication to their appointed
tasks is undeniable, as they tirelessly catalogue the vast array of
tomes and manuscripts, maintaining the sanctity of the temple's archives
with unmatched efficiency. Under their vigilant watch, the lower spire
remains a bastion of order and preservation, ensuring that the
accumulated wisdom of the ages endures for generations to come, while
enforcing strict protocols to safeguard against any threat to its
sanctity. Those who seek guidance in navigating the labyrinthine depths
of knowledge may turn to these silent sentinels, who possess an uncanny
ability to provide precise and instantaneous access to the location of
any desired tome, further attesting to their unfathomable mastery over
the arcane arts of organization and preservation.



Schools of Knowledge



Within the Temple of Knowledge, there are four main schools, each
branching into countless areas that expand with every new member. At the
helm of each school stands a headman, known as the Sacred Plume, second
only to the esteemed Highest Inkwell in rank. These schools encompass:



Arcanobiology à Delves into the intricate study of all living creatures,
blending arcane knowledge with biological understanding to unravel the
mysteries of existence. Through rigorous examination and exploration,
scholars seek to decipher the arcane forces that shape life itself, from
the smallest microbe to the mightiest dragon./


Archaeomancy à Specializes in the exploration of ancient relics, ruins,
and historical sites, seeking to unearth the secrets of civilizations
long forgotten. Scholars within this esteemed school pore over ancient
tomes, decipher cryptic inscriptions, and delve into the depths of lost
libraries to piece together the fragments of history. Through their
endeavors, they strive to illuminate the mysteries of the past and shed
light on the forgotten tales of bygone eras./


Elemental Arts à Is a vibrant haven where magic intertwines with
creativity, focusing on the study and practice of artistry infused with
elemental energies. Here, students harness the raw power of fire, water,
air, earth, lightning, heat, and cold to craft awe-inspiring spectacles
of expression. Through masterful manipulation of the elements, they
channel their inner visions into dazzling displays of harmony and
conflict, using their talents to paint the world with the vibrant hues
of magic and imagination./


Numeromancy à Is a realm where patterns, fractals, and symmetries
converge, empowering scholars to wield the fundamental mathematics of
nature itself. Delving into the intricate tapestry of numerical
relationships, students unravel the hidden language of the cosmos.
Through the study of repeated elements, clever symmetries, and
surprising geometry, they unlock the secrets of the universe, bending
its mathematical laws to their will. In this realm of esoteric
knowledge, numbers hold the key to unlocking the mysteries of existence.



Access to the Temple



The Oratorium --/> Free of charge for everyone.

The Hall of lore --/> Entry into the Hall of Lore for a day requires
either a payment of 10 gold pieces or a week of service to the temple,
assisting with various chores such as cooking, cleaning, cataloging, and
other tasks as needed. This dual system allows visitors to contribute to
the upkeep of the Hall while also providing an alternative means of
access for those who may not possess the necessary funds.



The sanctum of eternal wisdom --/> Restricted to members.
