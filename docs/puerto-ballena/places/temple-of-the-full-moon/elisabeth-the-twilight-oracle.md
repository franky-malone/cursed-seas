## Overview

**Elisabeth the Twilight Oracle** is a white dragonborn oracle who appears to have a close connection with [Sacred Plume Silica Stein](/factions/temple-of-knowledge/leaders/sacred-plume-silica-stein.md).

According to Silica, Elisabeth is one of the greatest specialists in **curses** currently living in [Norberia](/regions/norberia/).

## Golt's Curse

Elisabeth examined [Golt](/players/golt/) and identified the affliction affecting him as a powerful curse.

After studying his condition, she explained what she believed to be the possible ways in which the curse could be broken.

## Vision of the Crescent Wave

While in contact with [Jori](/players/jori/), Elisabeth experienced a vision in which she saw a group of **five individuals** who called themselves the **Crescent Wave**.

Following the vision, she asked Jori to visit her in [Puerto Ballena](/puerto-ballena/), hoping that by then she would have discovered more about the mysterious group and who they were.
