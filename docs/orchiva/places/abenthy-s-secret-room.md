This room is placed in a secret basement underneath the [Ringed Octopus](/docs/orchiva/places/ringed-octopus-inn.md) tavern. It contains several smut tomes, some magical items and the [Moonweaver's Lute](/docs/important-items/the-moonweaver-s-lute.md) and some poems of its creation.
