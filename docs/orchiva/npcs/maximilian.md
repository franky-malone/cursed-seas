## Overview

**Maximilian** is a resident of [Orchiva](/docs/orchiva/), widely regarded by the townspeople as the village idiot.

He apparently harbored romantic feelings for [Rosaline](/docs/orchiva/npcs/rosaline/), although it is unclear whether she reciprocated his affection.

Maximilian became involved in the theft of [Abenthy](./abenthy) old lute, which he stole on behalf of a traveling merchant named [Tobias LeClair](./tobias-leclair).

During the plague, he suffered from the relentless buzzing that tormented the inhabitants of Orchiva, leaving him in a state of severe mental distress.

## Feelings for Rosaline

Maximilian was apparently in love with Rosaline, the daughter of [Roy](./roy).

However, Rosaline had developed feelings for [Inus](./inus), and there is no indication that she returned Maximilian's affection.

## The Theft of Abenthy's Lute

Maximilian stole the old lute belonging to the famous bard [Abenthy](./abenthy) at the request of Tobias LeClair, a traveling merchant.

In exchange for the instrument, Tobias offered him **expensive alcohol and a book of love poems**.

The transaction connected Maximilian to the mystery surrounding Abenthy's possessions and musical legacy.

## The Plague

Like many inhabitants of Orchiva, Maximilian was affected by the plague.

He was particularly tormented by the incessant buzzing associated with the infestation, which subjected him to severe psychological suffering.

As the plague progressed, his physical and mental condition continued to deteriorate.
