## Overview

**Lucien** is the innkeeper of the [Ringed Octopus Inn](/docs/orchiva/places/ringed-octopus-inn), the largest inn in [Orchiva](/docs/orchiva/) and the only one known to remain operational during the plague.

With much of the city's leadership lost, Lucien appears to be one of the few surviving figures of authority in Orchiva.

He is also a bard and a descendant of the renowned musician and composer [Abenthy](./abenthy).

## Role in Orchiva

As the proprietor of the Ringed Octopus Inn, Lucien maintained one of the few functioning establishments during Orchiva's crisis.

His position made him an important figure in a city devastated by disease and the insectoid infestation.

## Abenthy's Legacy

Lucien is a descendant of [Abenthy](./abenthy), the famous bard whose compositions and hidden possessions remain part of Orchiva's history.

Following the defeat of the [Chitter King](/docs/orchiva/creatures/chitter-king.md), Lucien revealed that the lute previously believed to have been stolen was a fake. He also possessed the key to [Abenthy's secret room](/docs/orchiva/places/abenthy-s-secret-room.md).

Lucien subsequently guided the Farbound Fellowship to [Abenthy's Secret Room](/docs/orchiva/places/abenthy-s-secret-room), where they discovered clues concerning the [Moonweaver's Lute](/docs/important-items/the-moonweaver-s-lute.md), the [Belmont family](/docs/factions/belmont-family/belmont-family.md), and the [Cursed Islands](/docs/regions/cursed-islands.md).

## The Liberation of Orchiva

After the Farbound Fellowship defeated the Chitter King, Lucien named the adventurers **guests of honor of Orchiva**.

Although the sick inhabitants were beginning to recover, the city had suffered devastating losses, with approximately 30% of its population having died during the attack.
