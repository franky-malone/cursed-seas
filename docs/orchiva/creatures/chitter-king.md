## Overview

The **Chitter King** was a powerful insectoid creature encountered by the Farbound Fellowship during the events in [Orchiva](/orchiva/).

Identified as the **"chosen one of Obox'ob"**, the creature emerged from a larva shortly after Golt sensed a fiendish presence within the [Brooding Mother](./brooding-mother).

The Chitter King became the focus of a major confrontation that ended with the closing of a portal and the disappearance of the insects threatening Orchiva.

## Abilities

During the battle, the Chitter King demonstrated several dangerous capabilities:

- **Teleportation:** The creature could teleport, allowing it to escape the summoned wasps surrounding it.
- **Fear:** Its presence could instill fear in nearby creatures.

## Battle Against the Farbound Fellowship

The Fellowship confronted the Chitter King as it emerged from the larva.

Summoned wasps surrounded the creature, but its ability to teleport made it difficult to contain. Kespien provided magical protection against evil and good, while Jori summoned his spirit Pavo as an elemental, successfully striking the Chitter King in flight.

During the confrontation, Unheilmeer displayed its hunger for blood. Golt eventually hurled the weapon at the Chitter King, allowing it to consume the monster.

Following the creature's defeat, the portal closed, the insects vanished, and the clouds retreated.
