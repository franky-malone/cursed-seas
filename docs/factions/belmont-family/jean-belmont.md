## Overview

**Jean Belmont** was the father of [Kespien Belmont](/docs/players/kespien-belmont/).

Jean and his wife, [Victoria Belmont](/docs/factions/belmont-family/victoria-belmont), were killed during the goblin raid on [Crosscove](/docs/regions/mauer-mountains/crosscove)  that left Kespien orphaned.

[Kespien Belmont](/docs/players/kespien-belmont/) inherited his father's **broken longsword**, whose blade bears the Belmont family crest.
