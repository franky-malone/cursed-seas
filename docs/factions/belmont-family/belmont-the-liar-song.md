*Belmont the Liar* is a popular sea shanty recounting the supposed adventures of [Manfred Belmont](/docs/factions/belmont-family/manfred-belmont) and his ship, the **Yellow Wolf**.

The song mocks Manfred's claims of having sailed to the mysterious **Cursed Islands**, portraying his stories of distant lands and fabulous treasures as nothing more than lies.

Its popularity played an important role in tarnishing the reputation of the [Belmont Family](/docs/factions/belmont-family/), with later generations inheriting the stigma created by the song.

## Lyrics

### Verse I

*On the edge of the map, where the ocean meets sky,*  
*The cursed islands loom, where brave sailors die.*  
*Whispers of doom, on the winds they would send,*  
*No soul who dared venture, returned in the end.*

### Chorus

**Oh, Belmont the liar, and the Yellow Wolf's flight,**  
**With treasures in tales, through the day and the night.**  
**But the townsfolk all knew, his stories weren't true,**  
**Just a web of deceit, under skies so blue.**

### Verse II

*With tales of mermaids singing, and treasures untold,*  
*Manfred spun his yarns, in the sailor's fold.*  
*Of pearls in deep waters, and jewels in the sand,*  
*His stories enchanted, across sea and land.*

### Chorus

**Oh, Belmont the liar, and the Yellow Wolf's flight,**  
**With treasures in tales, through the day and the night.**  
**But the townsfolk all knew, his stories weren't true,**  
**Just a web of deceit, under skies so blue.**

### Final Verse

*So heed well, ye sailors, the lesson we've found,*  
*In Belmont's illusions, truth's never around.*  
*In the lore of the waves, his legend did fade,*  
*A cautionary shanty, in the songs we've made.*
