## Overview

**Victoria Belmont** was the mother of [Kespien Belmont](/docs/players/kespien-belmont/).

Victoria and her husband, [Jean Belmont](/docs/factions/belmont-family/jean-belmont), were killed during the goblin raid on [Crosscove](/docs/regions/mauer-mountains/crosscove) that left [Kespien Belmont](/docs/players/kespien-belmont/) orphaned.

[Kespien Belmont](/docs/players/kespien-belmont/) still carries his mother's **scorched spellbook**, one of the few possessions he has left from his parents.
