The **Belmont Family** is an old family whose name has become infamous due to the tales surrounding the exploits of their ancestor, [Manfred Belmont](/factions/belmont-family/manfred-belmont).

Stories of Manfred's journeys aboard the [Yellow Wolf](/factions/belmont-family/yellow-wolf) eventually gave rise to the popular song [Belmont the Liar](/factions/belmont-family/belmont-the-liar-song), earning the family a reputation for exaggeration and dishonesty that has survived for generations.

[Kespien Belmont](/players/kespien-belmont/) is one of the family's surviving descendants.

## Family Legacy

The Belmont name is closely associated with tales of [Manfred Belmont](/factions/belmont-family/manfred-belmont) and his journeys aboard the [Yellow Wolf](/factions/belmont-family/yellow-wolf).

Whether those stories were true or embellished, they eventually became the subject of the popular song [Belmont the Liar](/factions/belmont-family/belmont-the-liar-song), which cemented the family's reputation as storytellers and liars.
