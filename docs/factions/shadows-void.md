## Overview

The **Shadows Void** is a secretive criminal organization believed to operate from the shadows of [Nersand](/docs/regions/nersand/).

Rumoured to consist of **thieves, smugglers, and other criminals**, little is known about the true structure or leadership of the organization.

Although primarily associated with Nersand, rumours suggest that the Shadows Void has gradually **spread its influence to other cities across Norberia**.

## Criminal Network

The true extent of the Shadows Void's operations remains unclear.

Rather than operating openly, the organization appears to maintain contacts and agents throughout the criminal underworld, allowing its members to acquire information and perform tasks without attracting unnecessary attention.
