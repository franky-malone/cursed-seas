## Overview

**Silica Stein** is the current **Sacred Plume of Arcanobiology** at the [Temple of Knowledge](/docs/factions/temple-of-knowledge/).

A renowned scholar of creatures, monsters, and magical biology, Silica is best known as the author of the **Bestiary Stein**, an extensive work intended to contain information about the creatures and monsters known to exist.

He is also the adoptive father and mentor of [Aeriff](../members/aeriff).

## Arcanobiology

Silica's field of expertise is **Arcanobiology**, one of the four Schools of Knowledge of the Temple.

His knowledge focuses primarily on living creatures, monsters, magical transformations, and unusual biological phenomena. Although highly knowledgeable within his field, Silica readily admits when a subject lies outside his expertise. In particular, he does not consider himself an expert on history and knew relatively little about subjects such as **Myth Coralis**, the **Ipletherion**, or the **Unheilmeer** when questioned about them.

He is familiar with the stories surrounding **Marsander** and knows that some Marsandians survived the catastrophe and continued living in hiding. According to Silica, expeditions to obtain living specimens from Marsander are particularly difficult because much of what remains there consists of dangerous monsters and shadows.

## The Bestiary Stein

Silica is renowned for writing the **Bestiary Stein**, a comprehensive work concerning creatures and monsters.

His expertise extends beyond cataloguing creatures. He is capable of examining unusual biological and magical phenomena and identifying the properties of unfamiliar organisms and substances.

When shown the unusual red lilies found in [Dorelta](/docs/regions/autumn-forest/dorelta), for example, Silica was quickly able to determine that, if properly treated, they possessed the ability to heal numerous illnesses.

## Relationship with Aeriff

Silica is [Aeriff's](../members/aeriff) adoptive father and mentor.

He takes a clear interest in Aeriff's activities and companions. When the Farbound Fellowship arrived in Longsaddle, Silica was eager to hear about Aeriff's experiences and his newly found friends in Dorelta, inviting the group to his home for dinner.

Aeriff also appears to have access to Silica's facilities. When the party later acquired a **wyvern egg**, Aeriff offered to keep it safely within Silica's laboratory while the Sacred Plume was occupied with other matters.

## Encounter with the Farbound Fellowship

Silica first met the group at the [Temple of Knowledge](/docs/factions/temple-of-knowledge/) and later invited them to dinner at his home.

During their conversation, the party questioned him about several unusual phenomena they had encountered during their travels.

Among them were the transformations affecting Golt and Count Virgula. Silica considered the possibility of studying Golt's condition to determine whether his altered appearance was caused by a curse or another form of transformation.

He also discussed the **Sentinel** with the party. Based on what they told him, Silica suspected that, if the entity truly existed, it might be of demonic origin.

## Investigation of Golt

Silica took particular interest in [Golt's](/docs/players/golt/) physical transformation.

Since curses were outside his own specialization, he consulted [Elisabeth the Twilight Oracle](/docs/puerto-ballena/temple-of-the-full-moon/elisabeth-the-twilight-oracle), a specialist in curses.

After examining Golt, Elisabeth suggested three possible ways in which his condition might be reversed:

- Finding true love.
- Finding an ancient Selûnite artifact known as a **Moon Pearl**.
- Using sufficiently powerful magic to obtain a **Wish** or **Miracle**.

Silica's involvement in the investigation illustrates his willingness to seek specialists from outside his own field when faced with a problem beyond his expertise.

## Assistance to the Fellowship

Silica used his position within the Temple to grant the group temporary access to the **Sanctum of Eternal Wisdom**, normally restricted to members of the Temple.

This allowed them to pursue several different lines of research concerning their respective investigations.

He also helped arrange a meeting between the party and [Halphanis Severus](./sacred-plume-halphanis-severus), the Sacred Plume of Archaeomancy.

## Temple Examination

Silica later participated as one of the examiners during the Temple examination attended by [Kespien Belmont](/docs/players/kespien-belmont/).

Together with [Halphanis Severus](./sacred-plume-halphanis-severus), [Koko Bananicus](./sacred-plume-koko-bananicus), and [Melrea](./sacred-plume-melrea), Silica questioned the candidates and listened to their answers before the examination concluded with a final question from the [Highest Inkwell Eulius Federreich](./highest-inkwell-eulius-federreich).

[Aeriff](../members/aeriff) was ultimately declared the winner of the examination.

## Dorelta Refugee Crisis

Following the events in Dorelta, Silica and the other Sacred Plumes became occupied with managing the arrival of large numbers of refugees in Longsaddle.

During this period, Aeriff was preparing for the Temple examination while also acting as an intermediary between Silica and the Farbound Fellowship.
