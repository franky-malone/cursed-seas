## Overview

**Ellipsis Virgula** was the former **Sacred Plume of Archaeomancy** at the [Temple of Knowledge](/factions/temple-of-knowledge/) and the mentor of the current Sacred Plume, [Halphanis Severus](/factions/temple-of-knowledge/leaders/sacred-plume-halphanis-severus).

A brilliant historian, linguist and archaeomancer, Ellipsis was particularly renowned for his knowledge of **Marsandian history**, ancient languages and the restoration of historical artifacts.

Unknown to most of the Temple, there was a simple explanation for some of his extraordinary knowledge.

Ellipsis was himself a **Marsandian survivor**.

His research eventually became focused on **Mythals**, **Myth Coralis** and the lost archipelago of **Fabros**. He abandoned his position at the Temple and disappeared while attempting to reach the legendary [Cursed Islands](/regions/cursed-islands).

His current whereabouts remain unknown.

## Scholar and Sacred Plume

Ellipsis was regarded as an exceptional archaeomancer.

His ability to restore damaged artifacts and decipher ancient writings was considered extraordinary, and he was fluent in a vast number of old languages and dialects.

He became particularly famous for his work on **Marsandian history**. His writings about the war remained essential reading for younger archaeomancers even years after his disappearance.

Ellipsis was known as a strict but kind teacher.

He also possessed a peculiar fondness for **beets**, which he reportedly ate almost every day.

---

## Halphanis Severus

Among Ellipsis' students was a young and impoverished scholar named [Halphanis Severus](/factions/temple-of-knowledge/leaders/sacred-plume-halphanis-severus).

Recognizing his potential, Ellipsis awarded Halphanis a scholarship that allowed him to continue studying at the Temple.

Their relationship gradually extended far beyond that of teacher and student. Halphanis eventually regarded Ellipsis as a close friend and father figure.

Even after succeeding him as Sacred Plume of Archaeomancy, Halphanis continued to consider his master's scholarly accomplishments greater than his own.

---

## Marsandian Heritage

Ellipsis publicly presented himself as a scholar of Marsandian history, but secretly he was himself one of the survivors of [Marsander](/regions/marsander-shadows/marsander).

He frequently spoke of his brother **Ponto**, who had raised him after the death of their parents and whom Ellipsis believed had died during the war.

The loss affected him deeply.

For years, Ellipsis concealed his true heritage, even using a magical ring capable of disguising his appearance and influencing how others perceived him.

---

## The Crescent Wave and Myth Coralis

During his research, Ellipsis discovered a Selûnite legend concerning an organization known as the **Crescent Wave**.

According to the legend, these champions of the Moonmaiden had once defended the mythical city of **Myth Coralis** from a great evil using the power of a **Mythal**.

Myth Coralis was supposedly located somewhere within the lost archipelago known as **Fabros**.

The discovery consumed Ellipsis.

Around the same time, songs concerning [Manfred Belmont](/factions/belmont-family/manfred-belmont) and his supposed voyage to the [Cursed Islands](/regions/cursed-islands) became increasingly popular.

Where others heard a sailor's tale, Ellipsis saw a historical clue.

He began collecting different versions of the song, believing that the Cursed Islands described in the stories might actually be the lost archipelago of Fabros.

---

## His Plan

After Ellipsis disappeared, [Halphanis Severus](/factions/temple-of-knowledge/leaders/sacred-plume-halphanis-severus) secretly searched his private sanctum and discovered correspondence between his mentor and a Marsandian woman living in disguise in [Gardis](/regions/gardis/).

The letters revealed the true extent of Ellipsis' ambitions.

He believed that Myth Coralis contained a Mythal powerful enough to amplify a spell capable of **turning back time**.

Ellipsis intended to find it.

His ultimate goal was to return to the past, save his brother and prevent **Queen Sanderia** and the catastrophe that destroyed Marsander.

Shortly before disappearing, Ellipsis wrote that he intended to travel to Fabros.

He never returned.

---

## The Woman in Gardis

Ellipsis' correspondence also revealed a close relationship with the Marsandian woman living in [Gardis](/regions/gardis/).

Years later, Halphanis attempted to find her while investigating his master's disappearance.

He discovered that she had died during childbirth.

Her son had survived.

The apparently albino child was in reality a Marsandian named **Dash Virgula**, the son of Ellipsis.

Halphanis adopted the boy and raised him under the name [Jhandril](/factions/temple-of-knowledge/members/jhandril-dash-virgula), magically concealing his Marsandian heritage.

Years later, Halphanis finally revealed to Jhandril that Ellipsis was his father.

---

## The Virgula Box

Ellipsis left behind a mysterious box bearing a large letter **V**.

Even Halphanis was unable to open it.

When [Count Virgula](/players/count-virgula/) eventually encountered the box, he instinctively placed some of his blood upon it. The box immediately opened.

Inside were extracts from [Ellipsis' diary](/documents/diary-of-elipsis-virgula) and the magical ring he had once used to conceal his Marsandian heritage.

The diary provided further clues concerning Ellipsis' investigations and disappearance, including references to:

- **Tom Lancil**
- The [Cursed Islands](/regions/cursed-islands)
- [Manfred Belmont](/factions/belmont-family/manfred-belmont)
- **Fabros**
- **Gaurion**
- The **Crescent Wave**

The contents gave Count Virgula further insight into what his brother had experienced after his supposed death and where his search may eventually have taken him.

---

## Disappearance

Ellipsis left [Longsaddle](/longsaddle/) without informing Halphanis or the Temple of his destination.

Evidence discovered afterward strongly suggests that he intended to reach **Fabros** and locate **Myth Coralis**.

Whether he succeeded remains unknown.

No confirmed account of Ellipsis' fate has yet been discovered.
