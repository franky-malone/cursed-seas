## Overview

**Koko Bananicus** is a sentient ape and the current **Sacred Plume of Numeromancy** at the [Temple of Knowledge](/docs/factions/temple-of-knowledge/).

As one of the four Sacred Plumes, Koko presides over **Numeromancy**, the study of patterns, fractals, symmetries, and the mathematical principles underlying the natural world.

## Appearance

Koko is an awakened gorilla who walks upright and possesses a calm, calculating demeanor.

His dark fur is partially covered by elaborate robes decorated with arcane symbols, mathematical formulae, and constellations. He wears a rune-etched monocle over one eye and an infinity-shaped pendant around his neck.

He carries a staff decorated with rotating, number-inscribed rings and abacus beads, while scrolls, wax tablets, and old tomes fill the satchel he carries with him.

## Role in the Temple

Koko holds the rank of **Sacred Plume**, making him one of the highest-ranking members of the Temple beneath the [Highest Inkwell Eulius Federreich](./highest-inkwell-eulius-federreich).

His field is **Numeromancy**, one of the four Schools of Knowledge represented by the Sacred Plumes.
