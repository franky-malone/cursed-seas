## Overview

**Melrea** is a Janni and the current **Sacred Plume of Elemental Arts** at the [Temple of Knowledge](/docs/factions/temple-of-knowledge/).

As one of the four Sacred Plumes, she presides over **Elemental Arts**, a discipline dedicated to the study and manipulation of elemental forces through magic and artistic expression.

## Appearance

Melrea has a regal and distinctly otherworldly appearance. Her garments flow around her in shifting colors reminiscent of fire, water, air, and earth.

Her skin glows faintly with changing elemental hues, manifesting as crackling embers, rippling water, swirling dust, and gentle currents of air. Her hair seems to float as though submerged underwater and is threaded with small crystals, feathers, and sparks.

Melrea herself levitates slightly above the ground. She carries a staff crowned by a crystal within which a miniature storm appears to rage.

## Role in the Temple

Melrea holds the rank of **Sacred Plume**, making her one of the highest-ranking members of the Temple beneath the [Highest Inkwell Eulius Federreich](./highest-inkwell-eulius-federreich).

Her field is **Elemental Arts**, one of the four Schools of Knowledge represented by the Sacred Plumes.
