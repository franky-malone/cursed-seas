**Jhandril**, whose real name is **Dash Virgula**, is a young Marsandian wizard and the pupil and adopted son of [Halphanis Severus](/docs/factions/temple-of-knowledge/leaders/sacred-plume-halphanis-severus).

He is naturally shy, timid and easily frightened, although his experiences with the party have gradually made him more confident and capable.

## Apprentice of Halphanis Severus

Jhandril was raised and trained by [Halphanis Severus](/docs/factions/temple-of-knowledge/leaders/sacred-plume-halphanis-severus), the Sacred Plume of Archaeomancy. Their relationship goes considerably beyond that of master and apprentice, with Halphanis treating Jhandril as his own son.

Seeking to give his pupil some practical experience, Halphanis sent Jhandril on an expedition with the party. Jhandril initially presented himself as a relatively inexperienced wizard, but during the journey he gradually became more confident and began contributing more actively to the group's efforts.

His knowledge obtained through his studies proved useful while travelling through the Wyvern Mountains, where he helped the group identify and avoid potential dangers. During negotiations with a group of dwarves, he also managed to secure **10% of their ore** for the party.

[Kespien](/docs/players/kespien-belmont) was particularly impressed by Jhandril's growth during the expedition and eventually gifted him a **Wand of the War Mage** in recognition of how much he had developed.

## The Expedition

Unknown to the party, Jhandril was considerably more capable than he initially pretended to be.

Halphanis had instructed him to conceal his true abilities and report the party's actions throughout the expedition. The journey was effectively a test designed by Halphanis to determine whether the adventurers were capable enough to survive the considerably more dangerous expedition that awaited them.

Despite following his master's instructions, Jhandril became genuinely attached to the group during their travels.

Shortly before confronting the wyverns, he approached [Count Virgula](/docs/players/count-virgula) and asked him for advice about how to keep a secret when revealing it might endanger someone he cared about. Before leaving, Jhandril asked Virgula for a hug and secretly placed a large diamond in his pocket.

After the expedition was completed, Jhandril finally confessed the truth.

He admitted that he had lied about his abilities and that he had been reporting their actions to Halphanis throughout the journey. He was deeply ashamed of deceiving them and explained that he had only been following the instructions of the man who had raised him.

The revelation angered several members of the party, particularly [Kespien](/docs/players/kespien-belmont). After discussing the situation among themselves, however, they decided to forgive Jhandril on the condition that he would not deceive them in this way again.

Jhandril agreed.

He then revealed another ability he had kept hidden and used a **Teleportation Circle** spell scroll to return the group directly to the [Temple of Knowledge](/docs/factions/temple-of-knowledge) in Longsaddle.

## The revelation

Jhandril eventually learned that almost everything he believed about his origins had been deliberately concealed from him.

His real name is **Dash Virgula**.

He is the son of [Elipsis Virgula](/docs/factions/temple-of-knowledge/leaders/ellipsis-virgula) and a Marsandian woman from Gardis named **Sandria**, making him the nephew of [Count Virgula](/docs/players/count-virgula).

Years earlier, while investigating the disappearance of his former master [Elipsis Virgula](/docs/factions/temple-of-knowledge/leaders/ellipsis-virgula), Halphanis discovered that Elipsis had exchanged letters with another Marsandian living in disguise in Gardis.

When Halphanis travelled there, he discovered that the woman had died during childbirth, leaving behind a pale child who was believed to be an albino. Halphanis immediately understood the truth: the child was not an albino, but the son of two Marsandians.

The boy was living in an orphanage where he suffered mistreatment from both the other children and some of his caretakers.

Halphanis adopted him.

Initially motivated by the connection to his former master, Halphanis soon came to genuinely regard Jhandril as his own son. Wanting to protect him from his heritage and the dangers associated with it, Halphanis magically altered the boy's appearance and memories so that he would grow up unaware that he was Marsandian.

Jhandril therefore spent most of his life believing that his magically altered appearance was his real one.

Halphanis then explained that the real reason he had insisted Jhandril accompany them on their expedition was [Count Virgula](/docs/players/count-virgula).

Halphanis removed the magic concealing Jhandril's Marsandian heritage and told him his true name:

**Dash Virgula.**

Overwhelmed by the revelation, Jhandril fled the room.

The discovery nevertheless created a new bond between Jhandril and Count Virgula. Having spent his entire life without knowing his biological family, Jhandril became afraid of losing them shortly after discovering they existed.

When Halphanis was later gravely injured and left unconscious after a failed ritual, Jhandril admitted this fear to Virgula. He was afraid of being alone: Virgula would eventually leave Longsaddle with the others, his father was still missing, and even Halphanis the person he had always considered his family might no longer be there for him.

Jhandril gave Virgula one half of a pair of **Sending Stones**, asking him to remain in contact and to inform him if he discovered anything about Elipsis.

## Current Situation

Jhandril remains closely connected to both the [Temple of Knowledge](/docs/factions/temple-of-knowledge) and the adventuring party.

Following Halphanis's severe injuries, he helped the party investigate his master's laboratory and the failed ritual that had left the Sacred Plume unconscious and cost him a leg.

While the party continued their travels, Jhandril remained in Longsaddle and became one of their principal contacts within the Temple. Through the Sending Stones, [Count Virgula](/docs/players/count-virgula) has continued to keep him informed of important discoveries and dangers encountered during their journey.

Despite having learned his true identity, Jhandril is still trying to understand what being **Dash Virgula** means and what place his newly discovered family will have in his life.
