## Temple Areas

The Temple is divided into three major sections, each offering progressively deeper access to its accumulated knowledge.

### The Oratorium

With its open-air design and sweeping arches, the **Oratorium** welcomes all who seek enlightenment, offering a space where minds can gather, ideas can flourish, and the pursuit of wisdom knows no bounds.

The Oratorium provides a platform for individuals to share their expertise and insights through talks and presentations. Prospective speakers must submit applications to the Temple for licensure, which, upon approval, grants them the privilege of conducting their discourse within the Oratorium.

Speakers are expected to uphold a set of simple guidelines established by the Temple, ensuring a respectful and inclusive environment conducive to intellectual discourse.

Talks held within the Oratorium are open to all who wish to attend, fostering a community of learning and exchange.

### The Hall of Lore

The majestic **Hall of Lore** is accessible to all who seek enlightenment.

Visitors are granted the opportunity to engage with the Temple's teachings and delve into its repository of knowledge, albeit for a modest fee to ensure the maintenance of the location.

Within its halls, esteemed members of the Temple lead classes and colloquiums, sharing their expertise with those willing to invest in their pursuit of wisdom.

Meanwhile, the meticulously curated collection of books and manuscripts offers a gateway to the boundless realms of learning contained within the Hall of Lore's storied shelves.

### The Sanctum of Eternal Wisdom

The **Sanctum of Eternal Wisdom** stands as the inner sanctum of the Temple of Knowledge, accessible exclusively to its esteemed members.

Within its sacred confines lies the very essence of enlightenment, where the vast majority of the Temple's knowledge is preserved and disseminated.

Here, higher-ranking members conduct regular classes, imparting their profound insights and guiding disciples along the path of understanding.

Study rooms, ensconced in a permanent silence effect to foster uninterrupted contemplation, offer sanctuaries for members to delve into the depths of their chosen subjects.

Moreover, the Sanctum boasts a meticulously organized library, sorted alphabetically by every conceivable topic known to mortal minds, ensuring that seekers of wisdom may access the accumulated knowledge of ages past with ease and precision.

## How Is the Temple Organized?

Within the **Sanctum of Eternal Wisdom**, the halls are filled with diminutive automatons known as [**Modrons**](/factions/temple-of-knowledge/other-npcs/modrons).

They are whispered to be the ancient architects of the Temple itself, tasked with the sacred duty of gathering and safeguarding the repository of knowledge within its halls.

The mysteries surrounding these enigmatic constructs abound, as none can fathom the precise nature of their origins or the purpose behind their meticulous labors. Yet their unwavering dedication to their appointed tasks is undeniable.

They tirelessly catalogue the vast array of tomes and manuscripts, maintaining the sanctity of the Temple's archives with unmatched efficiency. Under their vigilant watch, the lower spire remains a bastion of order and preservation, ensuring that the accumulated wisdom of the ages endures for generations to come while enforcing strict protocols to safeguard against any threat to its sanctity.

Those who seek guidance in navigating the labyrinthine depths of knowledge may turn to these silent sentinels. The Modrons possess an uncanny ability to provide precise and instantaneous directions to the location of any desired tome, further attesting to their unfathomable mastery over the arts of organization and preservation.

## Schools of Knowledge

Within the Temple of Knowledge, there are **four main schools**, each branching into countless areas that expand with every new member.

At the helm of each school stands a head, known as a **Sacred Plume**, second only to the esteemed **Highest Inkwell** in rank.

### Arcanobiology

**Arcanobiology** delves into the intricate study of all living creatures, blending arcane knowledge with biological understanding to unravel the mysteries of existence.

Through rigorous examination and exploration, scholars seek to decipher the arcane forces that shape life itself, from the smallest microbe to the mightiest dragon.

### Archaeomancy

**Archaeomancy** specializes in the exploration of ancient relics, ruins, and historical sites, seeking to unearth the secrets of civilizations long forgotten.

Scholars within this esteemed school pore over ancient tomes, decipher cryptic inscriptions, and delve into the depths of lost libraries to piece together the fragments of history.

Through their endeavors, they strive to illuminate the mysteries of the past and shed light on the forgotten tales of bygone eras.

### Elemental Arts

**Elemental Arts** is a vibrant discipline where magic intertwines with creativity, focusing on the study and practice of artistry infused with elemental energies.

Here, students harness the raw power of fire, water, air, earth, lightning, heat, and cold to craft awe-inspiring spectacles of expression.

Through masterful manipulation of the elements, they channel their inner visions into dazzling displays of harmony and conflict, using their talents to paint the world with the vibrant hues of magic and imagination.

### Numeromancy

**Numeromancy** is a realm where patterns, fractals, and symmetries converge, empowering scholars to wield the fundamental mathematics of nature itself.

Delving into the intricate tapestry of numerical relationships, students unravel the hidden language of the cosmos.

Through the study of repeated elements, clever symmetries, and surprising geometry, they unlock the secrets of the universe, bending its mathematical laws to their will.

In this realm of esoteric knowledge, numbers hold the key to unlocking the mysteries of existence.

## Access to the Temple

| Area | Access |
|---|---|
| **The Oratorium** | Free of charge and open to everyone. |
| **The Hall of Lore** | **10 gp per day**, or **one week of service** to the Temple. |
| **The Sanctum of Eternal Wisdom** | Restricted to Temple members. |

### Service at the Hall of Lore

Those unable or unwilling to pay the entrance fee may instead dedicate **one week of service** to the Temple.

Such service may include:

- Cooking
- Cleaning
- Cataloguing
- General maintenance
- Other tasks required by the Temple

This system allows visitors to contribute to the upkeep of the Hall while providing an alternative means of access for those who may not possess the necessary funds.

## Ranks of the Temple

The hierarchy of the Temple progresses through several ranks, culminating in the **Highest Inkwell**.

### Lore Scribe

A Lore Scribe is a recognized scholar of the Temple of Knowledge and the first formal rank within its academic hierarchy. Candidates must contribute an original piece of knowledge in their chosen field and successfully pass an examination after their work has been reviewed by senior members of the Temple. Lore Scribes gain access to advanced Temple resources, including limited access to the Sanctum of Eternal Wisdom, and are expected to continue contributing new knowledge to the Temple each year.

### Chronicle Seeker

A Chronicle Seeker is an experienced scholar who has demonstrated the ability to produce several meaningful contributions to their field, including research undertaken at the request of a senior member of the Temple.
They enjoy greater access to the Temple's restricted resources than Lore Scribes, but must continue their research and undergo a yearly Proof of Knowledge conducted by their mentor to retain their rank.

### Custodian of Secrets

A Custodian of Secrets is a senior scholar who combines academic expertise with teaching and mentorship. Reaching this rank requires an established body of original research, completing work assigned by a Sacred Plume, and demonstrating the ability to teach others.
Custodians are expected to help preserve and transmit the Temple's accumulated knowledge. They must mentor at least one lower ranking member and periodically prove their expertise before the Sacred Plume responsible for their School of Knowledge.

### Sacred Plumes

Only **four Sacred Plumes** may hold the title at any given time, with one presiding over each of the Temple's Schools of Knowledge.
