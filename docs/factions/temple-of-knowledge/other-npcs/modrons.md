## Overview

**Modrons** are diminutive mechanical beings found throughout the [Temple of Knowledge](/docs/factions/temple-of-knowledge/).

They tirelessly work within its halls, gathering, organizing, cataloguing, and safeguarding the vast repository of knowledge kept within the Temple.

Despite their constant presence, remarkably little is known about them.

## Architects of the Temple

Some within the Temple whisper that the Modrons are not merely its servants, but its **original architects**.

According to these stories, the strange constructs may have been responsible for building the Temple itself long before its current inhabitants came to occupy its halls.

Whether there is any truth to these claims remains unknown.
