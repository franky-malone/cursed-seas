## Overview

**Telrond Whiterose** was a mysterious man encountered at the **Traveller's Inn** in [Dorelta](/docs/dorelta/).

## Encounter at the Traveller's Inn

The party encountered Telrond Whiterose while staying at the **Traveller's Inn** in [Dorelta](/docs/dorelta/).

His presence immediately attracted suspicion.

[Count Virgula](/docs/players/count-virgula) questioned him about his state of life, while [Golt](/docs/players/golt/) sensed that something about the stranger was unusual.

Realizing that the group had become suspicious of him, Telrond quickly left the inn.

## The Red Lilies

Further investigation revealed that Telrond had come to Dorelta in search of the unusual **red lilies** that blossomed there only once a year.

Another peculiar detail was the extraordinarily strong **rose perfume** he wore.

The fragrance appeared to serve a practical purpose: beneath the overwhelming smell of roses was a **rancid odour** that Telrond was apparently attempting to conceal.

---

## True Identity

The party eventually discovered that **Telrond Whiterose did not truly exist**.

He was a false identity used by [Master Gloran Herzblatt](./master-gloran-herzblatt), a supposedly deceased member of the Herzblatt family who had survived as an undead creature.

Gloran had been operating beneath the **Herzblatt family crypt** and was ultimately revealed to be responsible for the murders taking place throughout Dorelta.
