title: "Knowledge's Temple"
sidebar_position: 1
---

A large library temple located in Longsaddle. It has an area open to the
public and non-members and an area restricted to members only. Whoever
is seeking any kind of knowledge, has to try their luck in the
Knowlede's temple.



In order to grant access to the more private parts, one has to either
make a unique contribution to the temple, be a member of it or get a
direct invitation by one of its higher up members.
