## Overview

**Lory Swiftwind** is a halfling rogue who played an important role in [Kespien Belmont's](/players/kespien-belmont/) training.

While Dorn Firember taught Kespien discipline and combat, Lory taught him to compensate for his lack of physical strength by relying on **agility, speed, and precision**.

These lessons eventually became an important part of Kespien's fighting style and influenced the development of his own **Bladesong**.

---

## Appearance

Lory is small and agile, with mischievous eyes that constantly seem to assess her surroundings. She has a quick, sly smile and moves with grace and stealth.

Her clothing is practical and dark, allowing her to blend easily
