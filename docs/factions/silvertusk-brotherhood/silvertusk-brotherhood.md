## Overview

The **Silvertusk Brotherhood** is a renowned mercenary organization known throughout [Norberia](/regions/norberia/) for the crucial role it played during the continent's great war against **Marsander**.

Their actions during the conflict helped the allied nations turn the tide of the war and ultimately secure victory.

Today, the Brotherhood operates under the command of **Guludur Silvertusk**.

## The Silvertusk Brotherhood Today

Following the war, the Silvertusk Brotherhood continued operating as a **mercenary company**.

Its members make their living by accepting contracts throughout Norberia. Their work commonly includes protecting caravans, hunting dangerous creatures, and dealing with monsters that threaten settlements.

Rather than travelling as a single force, the Brotherhood operates through **small cells scattered across Norberia**.

These groups act with a degree of independence while ultimately reporting to **Guludur Silvertusk**, the leader of the organization.

---

## Legacy of the War

The Brotherhood's reputation is closely tied to its actions during the great war against Marsander.

Their contribution to the allied war effort earned the organization considerable recognition throughout Norberia, and the name **Silvertusk Brotherhood** remains associated with the conflict generations later.
