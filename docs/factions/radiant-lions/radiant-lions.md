---
title: "Radiant lions"
sidebar_position: 1
---

Order of paladins, clerics and men-at-arms with a strict moral code who
have their headquarters in Nersand. It is common to see solitary
individuals as well as patrols in the vicinity of the Prime Road on some
special mission for the city. They are often called religious fanatics,
although they always put laws and justice before any other action.
