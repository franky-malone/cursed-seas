## Overview

**Aurora the Elder** is the elder of a troupe of **Wanderers** encountered by the adventurers while travelling toward [Longsaddle](/docs/longsaddle/).

After a disturbing fortune reading by [Lapislazuli](./lapislazuli), Aurora told the group the [Tale of the Sentinel](/docs/documents/tale-of-the-sentinel).

She warned them that the Sentinel was not a name that should be spoken lightly.

## The Tale of the Sentinel

When [Lapislazuli](./lapislazuli) collapsed while reading [Golt's](/docs/players/golt/) fortune and declared that **"The Sentinel is here,"** she asked Aurora to explain what the words meant.

Aurora told the adventurers about [The Sentinel](/docs/players/golt/the-sentinel), a mysterious and dangerous figure whose appearances are associated with destruction.

She also warned the group **not to speak the Sentinel's name freely**.

## Abenthy

Aurora was not the original source of the tale.

She explained that she had heard the story from the majestic bard [Abenthy](/docs/orchiva/npcs/abenthy/), who had passed the tale on to her.

Through Aurora, Abenthy's story became the adventurers' first introduction to the legend of the Sentinel.
