## Overview

**Devin Greenshadow** is a member of the [Emerald Watchers](/factions/emerald-watchers/).

He was encountered at the **Emerald Horse**, accompanied by several other members of the organization.

## The Red Fang Threat

According to Devin, the roads in the region have become increasingly dangerous due to a rise in attacks by goblinoid creatures belonging to the **Red Fang Tribe**.

He believes this sudden increase in activity is most likely connected to the awakening of a **green dragon** in the **Emerald Groves**.

The growing goblinoid threat has made travelling through the region considerably more dangerous, even along roads normally watched over by the Emerald Watchers.
