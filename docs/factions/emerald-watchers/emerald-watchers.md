---
title: "Emerald watchers"
sidebar_position: 1
---

Organization of scouts and rangers who patrol the Prime Road between
Nersand and Orchiva, especially when crossing the emerald mangroves.
They live in the forests and outdoors and are responsible for keeping at
bay all the monsters that dare to come out of the mangroves. They act
under the orders of the Nersand council.
