Lory Swiftwind/
Race: Halfling/
Class: Rogue/
Characteristics: Cunning, resourceful, witty, independent/
Visual Traits: Lory is small and agile with mischievous eyes that seem
to constantly be assessing her surroundings. She has a quick, sly smile
and moves with grace and stealth. Her clothing is practical and
dark-colored, allowing her to blend into the shadows effortlessly.
