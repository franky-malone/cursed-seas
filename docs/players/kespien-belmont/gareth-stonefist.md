Gareth Stonefist/
Race: Half-Orc/
Class: Barbarian/
Characteristics: Strong-willed, loyal, protective, introspective/
Visual Traits: Gareth is tall and muscular with rugged features and
weathered skin. He has piercing eyes that reflect both his fierce
determination and his inner struggles. He wears sturdy armor adorned
with tribal markings, and his presence commands respect on the
battlefield.
