## Overview

Jori is a water genasi druid from [Mordian](/docs/regions/mauer-mountains/mordian) who spent most of his life among the local halflings, tending his kelp farm and helping control the population of Grail trout. His peaceful life ended when his mother, the marid **Undina**, contacted him through [Grail Lake](/docs/regions/mauer-mountains/grail-lake) and tasked him with finding **Myth Coralis**, the [Unheilmeer](/docs/important-items/unheilmeer), and its wielder.

---

## History

### An Unusual Birth

Jori was raised in [Mordian](/docs/regions/mauer-mountains/mordian) by his halfling father and paternal family. Although he always looked different from the other halflings, he grew up loved and accepted by those around him.

His mother was **Undina**, a marid who met Jori's father while he was working as a sailor in [Puerto Ballena](/docs/puerto-ballena/).

According to his father, Undina gathered kelp from [Grail Lake](/docs/regions/mauer-mountains/grail-lake) and shaped it into a small bundle. Together, they submerged it beneath the waters and wished for their love to bear fruit.

From that wish, **Jori was born**.

Undina disappeared beneath the waters of Grail Lake shortly after his birth.

### Life in Mordian

Jori lived a peaceful life in Mordian, tending his **kelp farm** and helping control the population of **Grail trout**.

From an early age, however, he felt a strange connection to Grail Lake. Whenever he was sad, he would retreat to its shores.

Sometimes, when looking at his reflection in the water, Jori could swear that **the eyes looking back were not his own**.

### The Druidic Path

After Jori began showing an affinity for druidic magic, his father introduced him to a druid who became his mentor.

His training centered around one fundamental principle:

> **Balance must always be preserved.**

Jori learned to understand the forces of nature, care for the ecosystem around him, and listen to the creatures and natural forces of the world.

### Dreams of Myth Coralis

Jori's mentor introduced him to the legend of **Myth Coralis**, a place that generations of druids before them had dreamed of finding.

Soon, Jori began having dreams of his own:

- Coral reefs displaying every color of the rainbow.
- Fish unlike anything he had ever encountered.
- Buildings and palaces made from sea-stone.
- Strange creatures swimming peacefully through crystalline waters.

The dream of finding Myth Coralis had passed from master to student for generations.

Now it had passed to Jori.

### The Call of Undina

Years later, Jori began hearing whispers in the waves and wind.

One day, while harvesting kelp, the waters of Grail Lake suddenly began swirling around him and dragged him beneath the surface.

There, Jori finally encountered **Undina**.

She revealed that she had never truly stopped watching over him. Whenever Jori had looked into Grail Lake and felt another presence looking back, it had been his mother.

Undina warned him that the **balance between their worlds was in danger**.

She gave Jori a mission:

1. Find the **Unheilmeer**.
2. Find its **wielder**.
3. Accompany them to the **Ipletherion** in Myth Coralis.
4. Ensure that they follow the right path.

For the first time, Jori had both a reason and the courage to leave Mordian.

### Leaving Mordian

Jori knew little about the location of Myth Coralis or the nature of the [Unheilmeer](/docs/important-items/unheilmeer).

Travellers had once told him of an enormous library in [Longsaddle](/docs/longsaddle/) known as the [Temple of Knowledge](/docs/factions/temple-of-knowledge/). If answers existed anywhere, Jori believed he might find them there.

Before leaving, his mentor asked him to visit an old friend in [Dorelta](/docs/dorelta/): [Miranda Greywolf](/docs/dorelta/npcs/miranda-greywolf), a fellow protector of nature.

A series of mysterious murders had taken place in the city, apparently connected to something of dark origin.

Jori agreed to investigate on his way to Longsaddle.

And so his journey began.

## Goals

- **Find Myth Coralis**
- Discover the nature and location of the [**Unheilmeer**](/docs/important-items/unheilmeer)
- Find the **wielder of the Unheilmeer**
- Reach the **Ipletherion**
- Help preserve the **balance between worlds**
