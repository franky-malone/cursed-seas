## Disappearance

While Golt was away on his mission to [Fin Island](/docs/regions/fin-island), his family was attacked in his absence.

Cassidy and her brother [Bant](/docs/players/golt/bant) were kidnapped by the **Shadows Void**.

Their disappearance became one of the defining events of Golt's life. After eventually returning home, he began searching for his missing children and continued doing so throughout his travels.

Years later, Golt even contacted the Shadows Void themselves and paid them to search for Cassidy and Bant and magically contact him if they discovered their whereabouts.

---

## Reappearance

Five years after her disappearance, Golt finally saw Cassidy again.

During the arrival of [Amirah Sephira Al-Marish](/docs/amirah-s-domains/amirah-sephira-al-marish) at **Ralto's Bazaar**, Cassidy appeared among the young women accompanying Amirah's procession.

She was dressed in blue robes and wore what appeared to be **magical cuffs around her hands**.

Cassidy appeared unhappy.

The moment Golt recognized his daughter, he desperately tried to reach her, forcing his way through the crowd toward Amirah's entourage.

He was too late.

Cassidy passed through a portal before Golt could reach her. Golt attempted to gain Amirah's attention and pleaded for an audience, but she continued through the portal with the rest of her entourage.

Although Golt failed to rescue his daughter, the encounter answered one question that had haunted him for five years:

**Cassidy was alive.**

Her exact circumstances, why she is with Amirah, and what happened to her during the years following her kidnapping remain unknown.
