## Overview

**Golt** is a former member of the [Radiant Lions](/factions/radiant-lions/) of [Nersand](/regions/nersand/), once a devoted follower of **Selûne** who swore to protect those who wandered beneath the moon and stars.

His life changed after a mission at sea ended with his capture by Sahuagin. Their rituals transformed his body and left large black runes embedded in his chest. When his prayers to Selûne went unanswered, his faith collapsed.

By the time he returned home, his children had been kidnapped by the **Shadows Void**. Soon afterwards, his wife [Alara](/players/golt/alara-deceased-wife) took her own life with the strange spear Golt had carried back from captivity.

That weapon was the [Unheilmeer](/important-items/unheilmeer).

With his family gone and his faith shattered, Golt began wandering [Norberia](/regions/norberia/), increasingly consumed by the idea that only greater power could prevent him from ever losing anything again.

Then something answered.

## Background

### A Radiant Lion of Nersand

Before his transformation, Golt lived in [Nersand](/regions/nersand/) and served among the [Radiant Lions](/factions/radiant-lions/).

During his entry into the order, [Sir Valendale](/factions/radiant-lions/sir-valendale) led his physical training.

Golt followed **Selûne**, regarding the goddess as the guide of his steps. He swore himself to the protection of those who travelled beneath the moon and stars.

As a Radiant Lion, Golt helped defend the borders with [Marsander](/regions/marsander-shadows/marsander) against the creatures that crossed into Nersand.

During these years, he also became familiar with the criminal underworld. One criminal in particular repeatedly escaped justice, leaving behind the initials **T. L.** after stealing valuable objects that would later appear on the black market.

Golt never managed to catch them.

### Alara and the Children

While living in Nersand, Golt met [Alara](/players/golt/alara-deceased-wife), a woman originally from [Dorelta](/dorelta/) who had recently moved to Nersand.

They fell in love and eventually started a family together.

They had two children:

- [Bant](/players/golt/bant), their son.
- [Cassidy](/players/golt/cassidy), their daughter.

For a time, Golt had everything he had sworn to protect: a family, a home, a purpose and his faith.

Then he was sent away.

### The Voyage to Fin Island

Golt was called to take part in a sea voyage to [Fin Island](/regions/fin-island) in order to deal with conflicts involving pirates operating around the island.

The ship never completed its mission.

During the voyage, it was attacked by **Sahuagin**. Among them was a warrior wielding an unusually powerful magical spear.

Golt and his companions fought back, but they were defeated.

For reasons Golt did not understand, the Sahuagin did not kill all of their prisoners.

Golt was one of those kept alive.

### Captivity and Transformation

The Sahuagin brought Golt somewhere he could not identify and subjected him to prolonged torture and strange sacrilegious rituals.

As part of those rituals, he was forced to drink a mysterious black liquid.

It changed him.

His body transformed into the monstrous form he carries today, and large black runes became embedded in his chest.

Throughout his captivity, Golt prayed to **Selûne** for help.

No answer came.

For someone who had spent his life trusting the Moonmaiden to guide his steps, her silence became as painful as the torture itself.

Eventually, however, the rituals gave Golt abilities he had never possessed before.

He used them to escape.

During his flight, he caught the Sahuagin wielding the magical spear by surprise, defeated him and took the weapon for himself.

Golt remembers little about what happened afterwards. He does not know exactly how he reached the shore.

He remembers only an overwhelming emptiness where the presence of Selûne had once been and an inexplicable certainty that, somehow, he would survive.

The spear came with him.

### The Fall of His Family

When Golt finally returned home, he discovered that his nightmare had continued in his absence.

His home had been raided by the **Shadows Void**.

[Bant](/players/golt/bant) and [Cassidy](/players/golt/cassidy) had been kidnapped.

[Alara](/players/golt/alara-deceased-wife) had been left alone to endure the disappearance of her husband and children.

When Golt finally stood before her again, she did not recognize the transformed creature that had returned in her husband's place.

Golt began searching for their children, but Alara had been devastated by everything she had lost.

She eventually took her own life using the spear Golt had brought home.

Golt lost control.

In the chaos that followed, he accidentally burned everything around him.

When it was over, almost nothing remained of the life he had once known.

Golt took Alara's ashes and decided to bring her back to [Dorelta](/dorelta/), the city where she had been born.

### Wandering Norberia

After losing his family, Golt wandered [Norberia](/regions/norberia/).

The happiness of the people around him increasingly left him numb. His thoughts instead turned toward power.

He had trusted a goddess.

He had sworn an oath.

He had protected others.

And when he had needed help himself, he believed Selûne had abandoned him.

Golt began imagining what it would be like to possess enough power to confront the goddess who had left him to his misery.

More importantly, he wanted enough power that he would never again be helpless while something he loved was taken from him.

It was during this period that he began hearing voices.

They promised exactly what he wanted.

Power.

The voices asked only one thing in return.

The spear he carried — a weapon they called the [Unheilmeer](/important-items/unheilmeer) — had to be brought to a place called the **Ipletherion**.

With Alara's ashes still in his possession, Golt therefore had two paths before him: fulfil his final promise to his wife and bring her home to Dorelta, and decide what to do about the increasingly tempting offer whispered through the Unheilmeer.

Eventually, his wandering brought him together with the **Farbound Fellowship**.

## Alara's Farewell

Golt eventually succeeded in bringing [Alara](/players/golt/alara-deceased-wife) home.

In the [Herzblatt District](/dorelta/places/herzblatt-district) of [Dorelta](/dorelta/), the party reached the sacred garden of the Herzblatt family.

There, among the red lilies and beside a fountain dedicated to Selûne, Golt encountered Alara's spirit one final time.

He promised her that he would find their children.

Golt then scattered her ashes in the garden as he had promised and said his final farewell to his wife.

Among her ashes, he found a ring.

---

## The Unheilmeer

The [Unheilmeer](/important-items/unheilmeer) has remained at the center of Golt's story ever since his escape from the Sahuagin.

It is far more than a magical spear.

The weapon appears to contain conflicting influences and consciousnesses, and it has repeatedly communicated desires of its own. At times it has demanded blood or souls, while at others Golt has heard contradictory voices pulling him in different directions.

Its history also appears to extend far beyond Golt himself.

### Dagon and Lathor

The voices Golt heard eventually led him to [Dagon](/players/golt/dagon), a powerful entity known as the **Prince of the Depths**.

[Lathor](/players/golt/lathor) acts as an emissary of this entity.

Golt entered into a pact with Dagon in exchange for power. The price was the same demand the voices had whispered to him before:

Bring the Unheilmeer to the **Ipletherion**.

The relationship between Golt and his patron has never been one of simple obedience.

Golt has challenged and provoked Dagon, while Dagon has repeatedly made it clear that the weapon does not truly belong to Golt.

During one confrontation, Dagon showed Golt what he could become if he refused to fulfil their agreement: a nightmarish creature known as [the Sentinel](/players/golt/the-sentinel).

Dagon warned him that the Sentinel would be watching and could hunt him down if necessary to recover the Unheilmeer and complete the mission.

### The Three Threads

Golt eventually revealed the story of his transformation and the Unheilmeer to [Halphanis Severus](/factions/temple-of-knowledge/leaders/sacred-plume-halphanis-severus).

After magically examining the spear, Halphanis discovered three strange threads of destiny connected to it:

- A **silvery thread**.
- A **dark and fathomless thread**.
- A third thread that appeared almost severed and was pulling strongly away.

Golt associated the mysteries surrounding these threads with **Selûne**, [Dagon](/players/golt/dagon) and [Lathor](/players/golt/lathor).

Halphanis promised to investigate further.

## Selûne

Despite Golt's belief that Selûne abandoned him during his captivity, the Moonmaiden has continued to appear throughout his journey.

During a battle, Golt experienced a vision of his former goddess in which a great spiritual wolf appeared before him.

Golt approached the creature and mounted it.

Through the vision, he heard the words:

> *"I have never left, child."*

The [wolf](/players/golt/selune-s-wolf) subsequently became Golt's mount and a recurring symbol of Selûne's continued connection to him.

Later, when Dagon punished Golt by forcing him to experience the terror of drowning, Golt heard the wolf howl and felt a gentle pull trying to save him.

He understood it as Selûne reaching for him once again.

Golt has nevertheless remained conflicted. The silence he experienced during his captivity fundamentally changed his relationship with his former goddess, and he has not fully decided whether he can trust her again.

---

## The Curse

Golt's transformation has since been investigated by members of the **Temple of Knowledge**.

Eventually, [Elisabeth the Twilight Oracle](/puerto-ballena/places/temple-of-the-full-moon/elisabeth-the-twilight-oracle), a specialist in curses, examined him and identified his condition as a powerful demonic curse.

According to Elisabeth, she could think of three possible ways to reverse it:

- Find **true love** for what Golt truly is.
- Find an ancient Selûnite artifact known as a **Moon Pearl**.
- Use sufficiently powerful magic capable of producing something comparable to a **Wish** or **Miracle**.

Whether Golt ultimately wishes to return completely to the man he once was remains tied to the larger conflict between his old faith, his new powers and the person he has become since his transformation.

## The Search for His Children

Finding [Bant](/players/golt/bant) and [Cassidy](/players/golt/cassidy) has remained one of Golt's greatest motivations.

Years after their disappearance, Golt even returned to the criminal underworld that had taken them. He contacted the **Shadows Void** and paid them **100 gp** to locate his children and magically notify him if their whereabouts were discovered.

His search eventually produced an unexpected result.

### Cassidy

At **Ralto's Bazaar**, Golt finally saw [Cassidy](/players/golt/cassidy) for the first time since her disappearance.

She was among a court of young female servants accompanying [Amirah Sephira Al-Marish](/amirah-s-domains/amirah-sephira-al-marish).

Cassidy was dressed in blue oriental robes and wore what appeared to be magical cuffs around her hands. She looked unhappy as she walked among the other servants.

Golt immediately tried to reach her.

He forced his way toward the procession, but by the time he arrived Cassidy had already passed through a portal.

Golt pleaded for an audience with Amirah, but she ignored him and continued onward.

For the first time since the kidnapping, however, Golt knew with certainty that his daughter was alive.

And he knew who had her.

### Bant

[Bant](/players/golt/bant) was kidnapped alongside his sister five years ago.

Unlike Cassidy, Golt has not yet discovered what happened to him.

His whereabouts remain unknown.

---

## Return to the Radiant Lions

Golt's relationship with the [Radiant Lions](/factions/radiant-lions/) did not completely end with his transformation.

During his later travels, he visited a branch of the order and submitted himself to an investigation under a *Zone of Truth* to prove his identity.

He successfully convinced the Lions that he truly was their former companion.

Because Golt also possessed information concerning the criminal **T. L.**, whom he knew from his earlier years in the order, the Radiant Lions allowed him to remain a loose member of the corps.

## Goals

- Find and rescue [Cassidy](/players/golt/cassidy).
- Discover what happened to [Bant](/players/golt/bant).
- Understand the true nature and purpose of the [Unheilmeer](/important-items/unheilmeer).
- Decide whether to fulfil his pact with [Dagon](/players/golt/dagon) and bring the Unheilmeer to the Ipletherion.
- Decide whether he can trust Selûne again.
- Find a way to break his demonic curse.
