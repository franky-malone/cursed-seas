**Lathor** is a demon who spoke on behalf of the **Prince of the Depths** and with whom [Golt](/docs/players/golt/) made a pact.

In exchange for power, Golt agreed to bring the [Unheilmeer](/docs/important-items/unheilmeer) to the **Ipletherion in Fabros**.
