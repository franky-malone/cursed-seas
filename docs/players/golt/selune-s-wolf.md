**Selûne's Wolf** is a spiritual wolf sent to [Golt](/docs/players/golt/) by **Selûne**.

It serves as Golt's mount, but its significance goes beyond that. The wolf is one of the clearest signs that Selûne's connection to her former follower was never completely severed.

## Selûne's Intervention

Golt had once been a devoted follower of Selûne during his time with the [Radiant Lions](/docs/factions/radiant-lions/).

After being captured and transformed by Sahuagin, however, Golt became convinced that Selûne had abandoned him. He had repeatedly prayed for her help during his captivity and believed that she had never answered.

His resentment toward the goddess eventually helped drive him toward the power offered by [Dagon](/docs/players/golt/dagon).

Despite this, Selûne continued to reach out to him.

During a desperate battle, Golt experienced a vision in which he found himself standing before a great spiritual wolf.

He initially struggled with what the apparition represented, but eventually approached the creature and mounted it.

Through the vision, Selûne delivered a simple message:

> *"I have never left, child."*

The wolf subsequently became Golt's mount and remained with him as a manifestation of his unresolved connection to the Moonmaiden.

---

## Against Dagon

The wolf appeared again when [Dagon](/docs/players/golt/dagon) confronted Golt over his pact.

Dagon forced Golt into a terrifying vision of drowning while reminding him that the [Unheilmeer](/docs/important-items/unheilmeer) had to be brought to the **Ipletherion**.

During the vision, Golt heard the howl of Selûne's Wolf.

He then felt a gentle force pulling at him, attempting to draw him away from Dagon's influence.

The moment suggested that the struggle surrounding Golt is not merely about his pact or the Unheilmeer. Selûne continues to reach toward him even as Dagon attempts to pull him further into his service.

---

## Symbolism

For Golt, the wolf represents something he has still not completely accepted.

He believes Selûne abandoned him at the moment he needed her most. Yet the wolf's presence suggests that, from Selûne's perspective, their bond may never have been broken.

As a result, the wolf is both a companion and a constant reminder of Golt's unresolved relationship with his former goddess.

Whether Golt will eventually reconcile with Selûne remains uncertain.
