title: "Dagon"
sidebar_position: 5
---

He was a name that Golt saw in the Unheilmeer and spoke aloud. After
that he made a pact with the prince of depths to obtain power.



He offered Golt power in exchange for bringing the Unheilmeer to the
Ipletherion.



It seems to be a very powerful demonic entity which threatened Golt to
comply with the pact they did or suffer the consequences as some other
of his chosen before did.
