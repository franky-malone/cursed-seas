## Overview

**Count Virgula** is a mysterious Marsandian survivor who travels with the **Farbound Fellowship**.

For much of his journey, Virgula revealed very little about his past. He claimed to have survived the catastrophe that destroyed [Marsander](/regions/marsander-shadows/marsander), but the truth surrounding his identity gradually proved considerably more complicated.

His real name is **Ponto Virgula**.

He is the supposedly long-dead brother of [Ellipsis Virgula](/factions/temple-of-knowledge/leaders/ellipsis-virgula), former Sacred Plume of Archaeomancy at the [Temple of Knowledge](/factions/temple-of-knowledge/), and the uncle of [Jhandril, whose true name is Dash Virgula](/factions/temple-of-knowledge/members/jhandril-dash-virgula).

Virgula possesses an unusual connection to **shadow magic**. Among its manifestations are his ability to summon his beloved shadow hound, [Count Donut](./count-donut), and other strange phenomena connected to his altered nature.

---

## Ponto Virgula

Before he became known as Count Virgula, he was **Ponto Virgula**, a native of [Marsander](/regions/marsander-shadows/marsander).

Ponto was the older brother of [Ellipsis Virgula](/factions/temple-of-knowledge/leaders/ellipsis-virgula). After the death of their parents, Ponto became one of the most important people remaining in his brother's life and effectively helped raise him.

At some point during the conflicts surrounding Marsander, Ellipsis received word that Ponto had been killed in a skirmish near the border.

To Ellipsis, his brother was dead.

The loss devastated him.

Believing himself to have lost the last member of his family, Ellipsis eventually abandoned Marsander. He arranged for the smuggler **Tom Lancil** to take him through old tunnels across the border into **Nersand**.

Shortly after escaping, Ellipsis witnessed the catastrophe that consumed Marsander from afar.

Ponto, however, had somehow survived.

---

## The Fall of Marsander

Exactly what happened to Ponto during and immediately after the destruction of [Marsander](/regions/marsander-shadows/marsander) remains unclear.

What is known is that he survived a catastrophe that transformed his homeland into the shadow-ravaged region known today as the [Marsander Shadows](/regions/marsander-shadows/).

At some point, Ponto himself became deeply connected to **shadow magic**, eventually emerging as the figure now known as **Count Virgula**.

For a long time, Virgula spoke little about these events. To his companions, he merely claimed to be a Marsandian who had somehow survived the destruction of his homeland and had once lived in the countryside.

The full nature of his transformation and survival remains one of the mysteries surrounding him.

---

## Shadow Magic

Virgula's connection to shadow manifests in several unusual ways.

His magic frequently takes on dark or spectral forms, and he is capable of calling forth a shadowy hound resembling a chihuahua.

This creature is known as [Count Donut](./count-donut).

Despite his strange appearance, Donut appears to be extremely important to Virgula and accompanies him into battle.

Virgula has also demonstrated powerful destructive magic and unusual magical abilities capable of creating duplicates of himself and allowing him to move rapidly across the battlefield.

The exact origin of his connection to shadow remains uncertain.

---

## The Farbound Fellowship

Eventually, Count Virgula began travelling with the adventurers who would become known as the **Farbound Fellowship**.

His knowledge of Marsander and his unusual magical abilities have repeatedly proven useful during their travels.

At first, however, much of Virgula's identity remained hidden from both his companions and the people they encountered.

That began to change when the Fellowship arrived at the [Temple of Knowledge](/factions/temple-of-knowledge/) and met [Halphanis Severus](/factions/temple-of-knowledge/leaders/sacred-plume-halphanis-severus).

Halphanis immediately took particular interest in Virgula.

There was a simple reason.

Virgula claimed to be **Ponto Virgula**.

And Ponto Virgula was supposed to have died centuries ago.

---

## Current Goals

Virgula's journey is now inseparable from the fate of his family and the mysteries surrounding Marsander.

Among the questions still surrounding him are:

- What truly happened to Ponto during the fall of Marsander?
- What caused his connection to shadow?
- Why has he survived for so long?
- What became of Ellipsis after he left Longsaddle?
- Did Ellipsis ever reach Fabros or Myth Coralis?
- Can Ellipsis still be found?
- What role will Virgula play in the life of his newly discovered nephew, Dash?

The answers appear increasingly connected to the same destination toward which the Farbound Fellowship is already travelling:

**the Cursed Islands.**
