There are rumors that an individual with the name Virgula was seen in
Longsaddle. It might be Ponto's brother.

 

After chatting with Halphanis Severus, the current Sacred Plume of
Archaeomancy, it was revealed that Ellipsis Virgula was the former
Sacred Plume of Archaeomancy ahd Halphani's mentor.

 

He got obsessed by Mythals, Fabros (Cursed Islands) and Myth Coralis and
abandoned his position at the Temple to try to find them. He hasn't been
seen since then.
