---
title: "Crispy Woods"
sidebar_position: 3
---

These woods are located to the east of
[Northeaven](/docs/regions/northeaven/northeaven),
they are named like that because of the mix of ash and snow that covers
the land, causing the snow to solidify into a thick layer of dark ice
that crunches under the feet of those who try to cross it.

 

It's woods are full of yeti and ogres that come from the mountains to
hunt.
