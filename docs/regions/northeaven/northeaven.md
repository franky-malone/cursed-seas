Northeaven is situated at the northwesternmost tip of
[Norberia](/docs/regions/norberia/).
It received its name because of the richness of its lands both for
farming and mining. Living there was like being in heaven since live
came easy and richness quick.

But everything changed when suddenly one day what was believed to be
just a mountain to the north, erupted and covered everything in a dense
layer of ash and smoke that prevented livestock farming and caused
numerous diseases in its inhabitants due to the poor quality of the air
and lack of sunlight.

From that moment on, the newly discovered volcano was renamed to
[Northell](/docs/regions/northeaven/northell)
and the name of Northeaven remained as a sarcastic way to remind their
habitants of their old lives. Due to the strong volcanic activity, it is
an area rich in gems, specially obsidian, and rich ores.

Since the volcano erupted, the cult of Chauntea, the goddess of crops
and agriculture, took on a fundamental role in the development of the
city. May druids and clerics that worship the mother of crops try to
make the city more livable for their inhabitants.
