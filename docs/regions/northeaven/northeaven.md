**Northeaven** is situated at the northwesternmost tip of [Norberia](/docs/regions/norberia/).

It received its name from the extraordinary richness of its lands, both for **farming and mining**. Life there was once said to be like living in heaven, as food was plentiful and wealth came easily.

## The Eruption

Everything changed when what had long been believed to be merely a mountain to the north suddenly erupted.

The eruption covered the surrounding lands in a dense layer of **ash and smoke**, devastating livestock farming and causing numerous illnesses among the inhabitants due to the poor air quality and lack of sunlight.

</div>

</div>

The newly discovered volcano was renamed [Northell](/docs/regions/northeaven/northell).

From that moment onward, the name **Northeaven** remained as a sarcastic reminder of the prosperous lives its inhabitants had once enjoyed.

---

## Volcanic Riches

Despite the devastation caused by Northell, the strong volcanic activity has made the region exceptionally rich in **gems and valuable ores**, particularly **obsidian**.

Mining therefore remains one of the important resources available to the inhabitants of Northeaven, even as they struggle with the harsh conditions created by the volcano.

---

## The Cult of Chauntea

Since the eruption, the cult of **Chauntea**, goddess of crops and agriculture, has taken on a fundamental role in the survival and development of the city.

Many druids and clerics devoted to the **Mother of Crops** work to make Northeaven more habitable, helping its inhabitants endure the difficult conditions brought about by Northell's continuing volcanic activity.
