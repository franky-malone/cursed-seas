It is said that when the world was young the Misty Forest extended all
the way until Northell and the elves could travel from one point to the
forgan river to the mountains without leaving their forest. The actions
of humans and other races caused the forest to separate and now they are
two different forests.

 

Until today, the veracity of this stories can be confirmed, since there
are several elf families still living in Dorelta. It is the hometown of
some lesser noble elf families and many humans and half elves.

 

Within these forests there is the city of Dorelta.
