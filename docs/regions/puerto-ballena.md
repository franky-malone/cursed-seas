---
title: "Puerto Ballena"
sidebar_position: 18
---

Puerto ballena is a little black spot on the coast of Norberia. It is a
city full of pirates, who attack the ships coming from and to Nersand.



It is the hometown of the famous "Capitán Algazul", which inspired
several novels about his heroic feats, inspiring many children to start
an adventure career.
