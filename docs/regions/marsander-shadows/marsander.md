## Overview

**Marsander** was the homeland of the Marsandians, a people whose history was profoundly altered by a catastrophe associated with **Queen Sanderia**.

The catastrophe destroyed Marsander and transformed its homeland into the shadow-ravaged region now known as the [Marsander Shadows](/regions/marsander-shadows/).

Much of the history surrounding its destruction remains uncertain. However, the experiences of several survivors, particularly the brothers [Count Virgula](/players/count-virgula/) and [Ellipsis Virgula](/factions/temple-of-knowledge/leaders/ellipsis-virgula), have preserved fragments of what happened before and after the fall.

## The Fall of Marsander

Before its destruction, Marsander was caught in a period of conflict. Skirmishes took place near its borders, and the fighting eventually affected the Virgula family.

During these conflicts, Ellipsis Virgula received news that his older brother, Ponto, had been killed in a border skirmish.

Believing that he had lost the last member of his family, Ellipsis arranged for the smuggler **Tom Lancil** to guide him through old tunnels across the border into [Nersand](/docs/regions/nersand/nersand.md).

Shortly after escaping, Ellipsis witnessed the catastrophe that consumed Marsander from afar.

The destruction left behind the region known today as the **Marsander Shadows**.

## Queen Sanderia

**Queen Sanderia** is closely associated with the catastrophe that destroyed Marsander.

Her role in these events remained central to the investigations of Ellipsis Virgula, who dedicated much of his later life to finding a means of preventing the disaster.

Ellipsis eventually came to believe that the magical power of the **Mythal of Myth Coralis** might allow him to turn back time, save his brother and prevent Queen Sanderia and the destruction of Marsander.

The precise circumstances of the catastrophe, however, remain unclear.

## The Marsandian Survivors

Most of the survivors hide among the peoples of Norberia in fear of the cumulated hatred from the the other cultures, as many Marsandians are still up to today seen as a major sign of disfortune and bringer of catastrophes.

## The Legacy of Marsander

The destruction of Marsander continued to influence events long after the catastrophe. Many creatures lurk in the territoritories of the Marsander Shadows and from time to time some manage to escape towards southern locations creating havoc and chaos.
