---
title: "Cursed Islands"
sidebar_position: 2
---

Islands located somewhere south of
Norberia.
There is a popular song that mentions them. Its name inspires terror in
any seaman, since it is said that anyone who sails with the intention of
finding them never returns. According to many it is just legends, some
other believe that riches and treasures await for the one who find them
and make it back alive.
