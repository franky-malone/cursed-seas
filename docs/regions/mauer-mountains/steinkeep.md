More fortress than city, **Steinkeep** is a massive stronghold located in the heart of the [Mauer Mountains](/regions/mauer-mountains/).

It guards one of the main passages through the mountains, effectively serving as the western gateway between [Norberia](/regions/norberia/) and the lands beyond the **Everchanging Dunes**.

Steinkeep is also one of the most important border crossings for overland trade. Merchant caravans travelling from **Neu Samir** pass through the fortress on their way toward **Gardis**, **Everlor**, and the rest of Norberia.

Its strategic position makes Steinkeep both a vital trading post and a formidable defensive stronghold controlling passage through the Mauer Mountains.
