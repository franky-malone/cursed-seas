---
title: "Longsaddle"
sidebar_position: 16
---

The city is dedicated to knowledge and study. It is the home of the
"Temple of Knowledge" a prestigious library, which is said to contain an
answer to every single question.

 

Many arcanists and scholars come to Longsaddle with the intention of
being accepted as a member of the Temple of Knowledge or being tutored
by a more powerful wizard.
