title: "Neu Samir"
sidebar_position: 2
---

The largest city in the great desert of ever-changing dunes. In ancient
times there was a Samir, but it was suddenly swallowed up in the sands
of the desert. There were some survivors though, among them the great
Caliph, and they were the founders of Neu Samir.



Its main connection to the rest of the continent is by ship through the
Crystal sea and by caravan through the Mauer Mountains.



Unfortunately, the Sea of Crystal is extremely dangerous due to
its unpredictable currents that cause ships to run aground on the
numerous coral reefs leaving the caravans as the main source to
transport valuable cargo and paying the toll at Steinkeep, what is the
source to many disputes due to continuosly increasing prices.
