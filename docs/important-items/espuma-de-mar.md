---
title: "Espuma de Mar"
sidebar_position: 7
---

Powerful drug in the form of a blue poweder. It enhances physical and
magical abilities of them who consume it but it is highly addictive. It
is consumed by chewing the powder, which turn the gums of the consumer
black. According to the rumores, a high consume of the drug will make
your skin grow scabs that resemble barnacles.
