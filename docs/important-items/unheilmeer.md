## Overview

**Unheilmeer** is a mysterious magical spear currently carried by [Golt](/players/golt/). The weapon appears to possess a will of its own, with conflicting influences seemingly connected to **Selûne** and [Dagon](/players/golt/dagon), the Prince of the Depths.

Its origins and true nature remain unknown, but its history predates Golt's possession of it.

The spear is said to be forged from **metal as dark as the deepest waters**. Its presence has been described as unsettling, and it appears to be connected to several mysterious entities and events.

Both Dagon and Jori's mother have expressed the desire for Unheilmeer to be brought to a place known as the **Ipletherion**.

### Known history

One of the earliest known appearances of Unheilmeer is connected to [Frederick Belmont](/factions/belmont-family/frederick-belmont), captain of the Black Albatros.

Before embarking on an expedition to the [Cursed Islands](/regions/cursed-islands), Frederick travelled to [Gardis](/regions/gardis/) to attend to family matters.

When he returned several months later, his behaviour had changed considerably. Once cheerful and outgoing, he had become quiet, distant and contemplative.

According to [Halphanis Severus](/factions/temple-of-knowledge/leaders/sacred-plume-halphanis-severus), Frederick spent long hours alone in his cabin, staring toward the horizon as though searching for something only he could see.

Members of the crew also reported hearing him repeatedly whisper a single word:

> **Ipletherion.**

Frederick had returned carrying a strange spear called **Unheilmeer**, forged from metal as dark as the deepest waters.

He refused to allow anyone near the weapon, while Halphanis sensed an unsettling presence emanating from it.

Frederick eventually departed aboard the Black Albatros in search of the Cursed Islands. The ship was destroyed during a violent storm, and Halphanis became its only known survivor.

**How Unheilmeer survived these events and eventually came into Golt's possession remains unclear.**

## Golt and the Unheilmeer

Following his escape from captivity among the Sahuagin, [Golt](/players/golt/) began experiencing strange voices and visions associated with the spear.

The weapon appeared to contain **conflicting influences or consciousnesses**, sometimes demanding blood or souls and at other times presenting contradictory messages.

The voices eventually led Golt to [Dagon](/players/golt/dagon), a powerful entity known as the **Prince of the Depths**, whose emissary is [Lathor](/players/golt/lathor).

Golt entered into a pact with Dagon in exchange for power.

The price was a specific demand:

> **Bring Unheilmeer to the Ipletherion.**

Dagon has repeatedly insisted that the spear does not truly belong to Golt.

### The Sentinel

During a confrontation with Dagon, Golt was shown a terrifying creature known as [the Sentinel](/players/golt/the-sentinel).

Dagon warned him that the Sentinel would be watching and could eventually hunt him down to recover Unheilmeer should Golt refuse to fulfil their agreement.

This threat suggests that Dagon considers the weapon and its destination important, although his ultimate intentions remain unknown.

## Conflicting Influences

Unheilmeer appears to be influenced by forces associated with both **Dagon** and **Selûne**, Golt's former deity.

Golt has heard contradictory voices and experienced visions that suggest the weapon is more complicated than a simple instrument of Dagon's will.

The precise relationship between these influences remains uncertain.

### The Three Threads of Destiny

Golt eventually brought Unheilmeer to [Halphanis Severus](/factions/temple-of-knowledge/leaders/sacred-plume-halphanis-severus) for examination.

Through magical investigation, Halphanis discovered **three strange threads of destiny** connected to the spear:

- **A silvery thread.**
- **A dark and fathomless thread.**
- **A third thread**, almost severed, pulling strongly away.

Golt associated these threads with Selûne, Dagon and Lathor.

However, the precise identity and significance of each thread have not been confirmed.

Halphanis promised to investigate the matter further.

## The Ipletherion

The name **Ipletherion** appears repeatedly throughout Unheilmeer's history.

Frederick Belmont reportedly whispered the word during his final months aboard the Black Albatros.

Years later, Dagon demanded that Golt bring the spear to the same destination.

Golt's visions also connected the Ipletherion to **Fabros**, the legendary archipelago associated with the Cursed Islands.

Furthermore, **Jori's mother** has also expressed the desire for Unheilmeer to be brought to the Ipletherion.

Despite these connections, the nature of the Ipletherion and what might happen if the spear reaches it remain unknown.

The [Farbound Fellowship](/players/) discussed whether to continue pursuing this objective. They ultimately agreed to grow stronger, seek allies and gather more information before making a final decision.
