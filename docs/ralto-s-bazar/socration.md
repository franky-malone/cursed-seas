## Overview

**Socration** is a merchant who operates a shop in **Ralto's Bazaar**.

He specializes in **books, musical instruments, and spell scrolls**.

## Spell Scrolls

| Spell Level | Price | Availability |
|---|---:|---|
| Cantrip | 30 gp | Always available |
| 1st | 50 gp | Always available |
| 2nd | 120 gp | 80% chance |
| 3rd | 300 gp | 50% chance |
| 4th | 750 gp | 30% chance |
| 5th | 1,500 gp | 10% chance |

### Availability

For scrolls of **2nd level or higher**, the listed percentage represents the chance that Socration has a scroll of the desired spell available.

A check for a specific spell can only be made **once per visit to Ralto's Bazaar**.

> **Material Components:** If a spell requires consumable material components, the price of the scroll may be increased accordingly.
