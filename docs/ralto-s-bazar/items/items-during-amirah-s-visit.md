## Item Details

### Vial of Spellweaving — Level 2

*Requires attunement*

**Price:** 2,000 gp

Once per day, the vial allows its user to change **one known or prepared spell of 2nd level**.

---

### Gauntlets of Ember Grip

*Wondrous Item, uncommon (requires attunement)*

**Price:** 700 gp

Your unarmed strikes deal **+1 fire damage**, and you can ignite small flammable objects by touch.

Once per long rest, you can cast **Burning Hands** at 2nd level with a **save DC of 13**.

---

### Belt of the Agile Cat

*Wondrous Item, uncommon*

**Price:** 400 gp

You always land on your feet after a fall and take **half damage from falling**.

Once per long rest, you can gain **advantage on a Dexterity saving throw**.

---

### Boots of Wind's Favor

*Wondrous Item, uncommon (requires attunement)*

**Price:** 700 gp

While wearing these boots, your movement speed increases by **10 feet**.

Once per short rest, you can use a **bonus action to Dash without provoking opportunity attacks**.

---

### Bloodmire Phiale

**Price:** 300 gp

This shallow ceramic drinking bowl is enchanted to repurpose spilled blood into new life. The bowl can hold up to **8 ounces of blood**.

Once filled, the blood begins to slowly swirl on its own. If left undisturbed for **1 hour**, the blood becomes a gruesome tonic that pulses with a faint internal glow.

A creature that drinks the entire bowl rolls a **d12**:

- **12:** The creature takes **12 necrotic damage**. If this reduces it to 0 hit points, it is stable.
- **1–11:** The creature regains hit points equal to **4 + the number rolled**.

The blood is thick and sweet but leaves a bitter aftertaste. Its magic fades if it is poured from the bowl without being drunk.

Once the bowl has been used, it can't be used again for **1d4 days**.

---

### Restful Music Box

**Price:** 300 gp

This small, ornate music box plays a soothing melody that restores vitality to those who hear it during moments of respite.

During a **short rest**, you can wind and play the music box. Any creature that can hear the music and spends at least one Hit Die to regain hit points during that rest regains an additional **1d6 hit points**.

The music box can be used **once per day**.

If a bard uses the music box in conjunction with **Song of Rest**, the benefits stack. In addition, the extra die granted by Song of Rest increases by **one die size** according to the bard's Song of Rest progression—for example, from a d6 to a d8.
