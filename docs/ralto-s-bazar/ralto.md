## Overview

**Ralto** is an interplanar merchant who travels between planes aboard a gigantic snail.

Within it, he operates **Ralto's Bazaar**, where he keeps and trades a considerable collection of valuable magical items.

Despite his dealings with the party, Ralto's true nature remains uncertain.

## Ralto's Bazaar

The party first encountered Ralto as a merchant offering access to an unusual collection of magical items.

During their first meeting, he offered them a deal: **retrieve a valuable piece of metal for him**, and in exchange they would be allowed to choose one of the magic items he had in stock and gain access to his interplanar bazaar.

To contact the party, Ralto gave them a **small magical snail**, which he could use to communicate with them and let them know when the mission was ready to begin.

## The Reversed Hands

[Jori](/docs/players/jori/) and [Aeriff](/docs/factions/temple-of-knowledge/members/aeriff.md) noticed something strange when shaking Ralto's hand: his thumb appeared to be misplaced, almost as though the hand belonged on the opposite side of his body.

When the party later discussed this observation with [Sacred Plume Silica Stein](/docs/factions/temple-of-knowledge/leaders/sacred-plume-silica-stein), he suggested a disturbing possibility.

If what Jori and Aeriff noticed was correct, **Ralto could be a Rakshasa in disguise**.

---

## Possible Rakshasa

According to the information provided by Silica, **Rakshasas are powerful devils** known for their interest in accumulating magical items.

They possess considerable resistance to magic and are capable of reading minds. Unlike many other devils, their dealings do not necessarily revolve around acquiring mortal souls.

These traits, combined with Ralto's unusual hands and his collection of magical treasures, have led the party to suspect that the merchant may be hiding his true nature.

However, **Ralto's identity as a Rakshasa has not been confirmed**.
