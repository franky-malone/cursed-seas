## Overview

**Jhoras** is a merchant operating in **Ralto's Bazaar**, specializing in mundane adventuring equipment and supplies.

## Available Items

Jhoras sells **any item worth less than 25 gp** from the *Player's Handbook* Adventuring Gear, with the exceptions listed below.

### Not Available

- Acid
- Books
- Component Pouches
- Holy Water
- Hourglasses
- Jeweler's Tools
- Musical Instruments
- Living creatures
- Carts
- Sleds
