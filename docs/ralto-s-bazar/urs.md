## Overview

**Urs** is a legendary human smith from the Material Plane and a close friend of [Ralto](./ralto).

An accomplished craftsman and planar traveler, he journeyed across the planes in search of ores containing unusually high concentrations of **Yal**.

## Planar Travels

Urs frequently traveled alongside Ralto in search of rare materials and valuable ores.

At some point during these travels, he encountered a planar entity and became afflicted with a mysterious curse known as **Ursanthropy**.

Following a failed teleportation spell, Urs and Ralto became separated and Urs disappeared somewhere among the planes.

## Lost in the Shadowfell

It is believed that Urs eventually became stranded in the [Shadowfell](/shadowfell/shadowfell.md), where he may have remained for hundreds of years.

During this time, the curse of Ursanthropy continued to affect him.

---

## Legendary Smith

Urs was already considered a **legendary smith** before his disappearance.

According to Ralto, the golems that protect and serve within **Ralto's Bazaar** were originally created by Urs and gifted to him.
