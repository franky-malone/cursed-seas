## Month Spent on Downtime

The group spent most of the month pursuing their own activities in Longsaddle.

### Kespien

- Took part in the **Navigator's Tools** course. Unfortunately, he needed more time than expected, but nevertheless completed it successfully.
- Studied for the Temple of Knowledge exam with **Jhandril** and **Jori** as tutors, while also attending the Hall of Lore for several weeks.
- Commissioned a set of beautiful cloaks and brooches bearing the newly designed symbol of the **Farbound Fellowship**, which he gifted to the group the night before the exam.

### Jori

- Took the courses **"Secrets of the Marshlands"** and **"Forgotten Cults and Their Rites"**, learning about the Cursed Swamps, Kurz O'ktar, and a forgotten cult devoted to insectoid creatures.
  - **Cursed Swamps:** Traders avoid the region due to rumours of witches and hags. One of its greatest dangers is mosquitoes as large as doves (**Stirges**), which live in groups and spread disease.
  - **Emerald Mangroves:** Home to **Kurz O'ktar**, a city inhabited by monsters, including orcs, goblins, and trolls.
  - **Cult of Deep Sashelas:** An elven sea-god cult whose influence was eventually overtaken by Selûne and Umberlee.
  - Learned of a forgotten cult focused on the spread of insectoid creatures.
- Tutored Kespien in **Nature** and **Religion**.
- Crafted several healing potions.
- Carved the engravings for Kespien's birthday present.
- Spent time, with Virgula's help, searching for raw materials from which to create a non-metal breastplate.

### Virgula

- Took the course **"Undead Lore and Anathema"**, learning about different types of undead creatures and Dhampirs.
- Took **"Empires of Old: A Comparative Study"**, learning about the Norberian perspective on Marsander and the lost empire of Samir.
- Helped Jori search for raw materials.
- Spent quite a bit of time gambling and earned some riches.
- Heard several rumours:
  - A small coastal town near **Wolfsruhe** had been massacred by an unknown creature. All its inhabitants had been decapitated, their heads seemingly bitten off, and the town had been completely ransacked and pillaged.
  - The city of **Orchiva** is said to be cursed. Merchants have begun avoiding it, and some are concerned about the state of crops and food deliveries.
  - A noble elf from **Everlor** has been seen in Longsaddle.

### Golt

- Learned how to steer boats (**Water Vehicles**) and perform construction and repair work with **Carpenter's Tools**.
- Researched the cost of hiring a captain for sea travel. A skilled and honest individual would charge around **2–10 gp**.
- Visited a branch of the **Radiant Lions** and underwent an investigation under a *Zone of Truth* to prove his identity. He persuaded the Lions to allow him to remain a loose member of the corps because he had leads on the long-sought criminal **Tom Lancil**.
- Contacted the underworld, specifically the **Shadows Void**, and paid them **100 gp** to locate his children and magically notify him of their whereabouts.

## Exam of the Temple of Knowledge

Kespien entered the Temple trying to appear as confident as possible, though in truth he was very nervous. The examination room was mysterious and astonishing in equal measure.

He met several other contestants there, including **Aeriff**, who was unpleasantly surprised to see Kespien and took his participation as a personal insult to his intelligence. Kespien also met a Firbolg named **Zarzaparrilla**, a Tabaxi named **Hatu-khnum-amen**, and a Kenku named **Rabenax**.

The exam began in a darkened chamber, where five thrones stood dimly illuminated. A chair and desk appeared for each participant, accompanied by magical ink and quills.

The first Sacred Plume to appear was **Halphanis**, followed by **Silica Stein** and the other two Plumes.

**Koko Bananicus** was a sentient ape responsible for the field of Numeromancy. **Melrea** was an interplanar being capable of commanding the elements and presided over the Elemental Arts.

Each Sacred Plume posed their questions and listened to the contestants' answers.

Finally, the **Highest Inkwell, Eulius Federreich**, appeared. The golden, owl-like Aarakocra asked the final question:

> **What is the most valuable knowledge?**

Each participant took their time before giving an answer.

After a period of silence, the winner was announced.

Unfortunately for Kespien, **Aeriff** had claimed the victory. He could not hide his pride during his acceptance speech, which sounded rather arrogant to Kespien's ears.

The group met outside the Temple and tried to cheer Kespien up. Aeriff joined them, announced the news of his victory, and invited everyone for drinks at the tavern.

### Zyralis

While they were drinking, Aeriff noticed an attractive elven woman looking in Virgula's direction. She invited Virgula over, and he accepted.

She introduced herself as **Zyralis**, an elf from Everlor. She claimed to be very interested in Virgula and, particularly, in the exploits of the **Farbound Fellowship**, of whom she had somehow already heard.

She flirted heavily with Virgula, who was using his non-Marsandian appearance, and offered to bring him some Everlorian drinks.

Zyralis returned with an elaborate bottle containing a pale green wine. Virgula did not hesitate to drink it.

Unfortunately, he had fallen into her trap.

The drink contained a **Philter of Love**.

Once the potion took effect, Zyralis revealed her true intentions and began coaxing information out of Virgula. Virgula remained somewhat evasive and, at one point, returned to the others to confirm the information he intended to give her.

Zyralis was searching for what she called a **family heirloom**: the blade discovered in the crypts of the Herzblatt family, the **Espina de Otoño**.

The group suspected something was wrong and attempted to follow Zyralis and Virgula upstairs, with little success.

Eventually, Zyralis learned that the blade had been sold to an interplanar being named **Ralto**, whose market could be accessed through some kind of snail portal.

She escaped through a portal, but not before telling Virgula that the group had made an enemy for life and that she would reclaim what belonged to her.

Kespien dispelled the magic affecting a heartbroken Virgula, and the group discussed what had happened.

Jori remembered the name **Zyralis**. It was also the name of **Velen Herzblatt's wife**. The group had previously discovered a tomb in the Herzblatt crypts depicting a woman wielding a blade.

Whether this Zyralis is the same woman remains unknown.

The group spent the rest of the night discussing what had happened before finally getting some rest.
