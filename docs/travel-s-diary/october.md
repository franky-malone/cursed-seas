Thursday, November 30, 2023

1:37 PM

11 October (Day 1)

Kespien and Jori reached the depths of the autumn forest. Jori finally
connected with his totem spirits.

Aeriff also reached the forest.

The three of them found a wagon abandoned in the middle of the road with
no signs of driver or draft animals. Within the wagon there was a coffin
from where emerged Count Virgula. All of them were attacked by huge
spiders and an humanoid spider creature called an Ettercap. They
defeated them and effortlessly forcing the Ettercap to run away.

They also saved a man named
[John](/docs/dorelta/john)
and his horses from being the spiders next meal. After that they reached
Dorelta
before the closing of the gates and they seek accommodation in the local
inn. Presentation were made and drinks were exchanged. They learned of
the recent murder of a young elf noble and the curfew over
Dorelta.



Golt didn't make it in time to reach
Dorelta
and decided to wait before the gates until they were open.



12 October (Day 2)

The party met again and met Golt. After some discussions Golt decided to
join the party in their investigations for the time being. They read the
job post and decided to go talk to the local healer, [Miranda
Greywolf](/docs/dorelta/dorelta-npcs/miranda-greywolf).
Thanks to Jori they figured out what happened to [Morrik
Oaktree](/docs/dorelta/dorelta-npcs/morrik-oaktree).
His body was found dead by the mill and his heart was missing. They also
inspected the big statue of [Velen
Herzblatt](/docs/dorelta/family-herzblatt/velen-herzblatt).

They decided to go back to the travelers district before it was dark.
They asked some questions to [Brund
Redtile](/docs/dorelta/dorelta-npcs/brunn-redtile),
the innkeeper, about the local situation and decided to go to sleep.



13 October (Day 3)

They went to the townhall and tried to convince the secretary of the
mayoress,
[Felosial](/docs/dorelta/dorelta-npcs/felosial),
to let them pass. Thanks to Aeriff they managed to get a meeting within
the hour, but before it passed the mayoress [Rulias
Clearsong](/docs/dorelta/dorelta-npcs/rulias-clearsong)
came out and agreed to meet them earlier. She explained the situation
and agreed to give them a reward of 750 GP if they find out who did it
and why it did it. An additional plus of 60 GP was handled if they could
solve the murder before 3 days. The mayoress provided them with safe
passage after the curfew and promised them to allow to access the
Herzblatt district if they solved the problems.



They then decided to go to the nobles district and meet there the
Oaktree family directly. Before going in, they were stopped by two
guards. Kespien introduced himself as a Belmont, what caused the guards
to mock him, referring to something about a song. They also mentioned to
have heard it from a famous bard named Helene
Soprani,
apparently an acquittance of Jori. Thanks to the combined action of the
group, but mostly Golt, they managed to secure passage and get an
audience with the master of the house.



They met the Oaktree family after meeting with Mike the butler. There
they met Mr. and Miss Oaktree. While on the discussion, Kespien used
mental magic to inspect the thoughts of both individuals trying to find
some hints of what really happened. Even though he was discovered, Mr.
Oaktree forgave the intrusion but finished earlier the interrogation
session. He promised them 1.500 GP if they could find who did that to
his son and point the blame to Rulias Clearsong, the mayoress.
Apparently he had a fond hatred against that woman. The group then
decided to investigate Morriks room, where they found a love letter to
Deia Sweethoney, Morriks fiancée, and a first edition novel in elven of
"The adventures of Captain Algazul", which they stole.



They then decided to leave and investigate the mill, where Morriks
corpse was found. There they asked the miller what he saw or knew, with
little to no success. But they were able to get a glimpse of the
corrupted Herzblatt district on the north. After that they decided to
divide the party to try to gather as much information as possible.



Golt would meet with Lady Lucretia Oaktree with hopes of seeing Morriks
corpse up close but before went to the Sweethoney estate to try and meet
Morriks fiancée. Unfortunately due to his appearance he only was able to
meet the head of the Sweethoney's house, which refused to allow her
daughter to pass over more painful moments. Lady Sweethoney confirmed
the hatred of the noble elves for the human and demi human races of
Dorelta, specially the mayoress. He then met with Lucretia and was
allowed to inspect the body of Morrick. He found that the hole he had in
his chest had a very powerful undead aura on it.



The rest of the party travelled back to the Travellers District, but on
the way they were intercepted by Brakadanth, the faery dragon. He
presented himself as a big dragon, but it was only an illusion. After
some discussions, Brakadanth asked the group to help him with the so
called "spider people" which the group accepted. He also asked for some
sweets.



When they arrived to the Travelleres District, the party split again.
Jori and Count Virgula decided to go to the inn and see if the young
elves were there, which they were. They outplayed them in a card game
and tried to gain information of what happened with little success.



Kespien and Aeriff went to meet and help Felosial in the archives and
investigated about the Herzblatt family, their history and records about
their visits to Dorelta.



Golt made a stop in the outskirts of the Herzblatt district, where he
found out that the bridge that crossed the reiver was heavily closed and
guarded by two men. He tried to get some information out of them, but
got mixed statements. The younger claimed to have seen lights and heard
whispers and old one said that it might have been the big stirges that
sometimes roam the river. After that the decided to march before dawn to
the Travelers district.



14 October (Day 4)



Kespien and Aeriff had pulled an all nighter and decided to meet with
Jori and Virgula.



When Jori woke up, he found a mysterious note that was passed under the
door asking to meet them the next night outside the travelers district.



Golt came back and shared everything he had found out in the nobles and
Herzblatt district with the group and it was almost clear for all of
them, that whatever happened to Morrik was in the Herzblatt district.



While Golt, Virgula and Aeriff decided to go shopping, Jori and Kespien
went to meet the mayoress. After sharing what they have found until now,
she told a little bit more about the Herzblatt family. Apparently the
old mayor, the one who put her in charge, was somehow related to the
Herzblatt family and that is why there are still so much information
about them in the local archive. The Herzblatt, before the suddenly all
left, brought a coffin to their district in which they apparently had
one of their family members. The mayoress also told them that the
Herzblatt were by far not the necromancers the group thought them to be
and that they often put some safeguards and magical protection on their
district against evil magic. But that after they brought the coffin they
started to leave the city one after the other. With this new
information, the group requested a permit to enter the Herzblatt
district which the mayoress agreed to sign and give them. They also
managed to convince her to extend the time for the bonus in one day, but
also said that she didn't have the means to match the offer that most
probably Mr. Oaktree made them. After that they left to meet the others.



Once again together, the group decided to go help Brakadanth and his
fellow fey friends. They even brought him sweets and good berries, which
initially got ignored because of the yummier sweets but finally also got
eaten. Brakadanth brought them through the forest avoiding dangers and
passing by a part of the forest which wasn't corrupted and seemed to be
the tomb of an old friend of Brakadanth, Robin. The person who brought
him from the Feywild to the material plane. The group ignored it and
kept going until they reached a dome made out of webbings and bushes.
Kespien took the initiative and went in to set everything on fire. He
managed to do it without being noticed by the things inside and also
managed to set the whole thing on fire. Screeches were heard and spiders
of different sizes run out of the dome. After the ceiling collapsed, the
nest of the monsters was exposed. Some spider people, an ettercap and a
large spider with human hands monsters who seemed to be the one lying
eggs was the monsters to face. They fought harshly against them and turn
victorious.



After that they inspected the dome and killed all remaining eggs they
could find. They then met Brakadanth who was very happy and gave them a
reward. A big and heavy golden acorn that belonged to Robin and Jori
happily accepted, since it would allow him to call on the fey creatures
to aid them in his journey. Aeriff asked Brakadanth to leave the forest
and join them, but he refused.



Instead another faerie dragon, with red scales named Vermelho, decided
to join them. After that they decided to go back to the traveler's
district, rest a bit and meet at night with the mysterious person who
seemed to know what happened to Morrik.



When they reached the Inn they found John meeting with a strange man who
introduced himself as Telrond Whiterose, a rather old elf wearing
herbalist robes. He was wearing a very strong roses perfume, but Virgula
noticed that he was trying to mask a rather rancid smell. After Golt
used his divine sense, he noticed that this individual had an undead
aura. He then immediately left the inn rather offended.



The group asked John about what they were talking and also Brund to see
if they knew him and the purpose of his visit. They found out that he
was seeking the famous red lilies of Dorelta, which were told to blossom
once a year and have healing properties. Nobody had heard before of that
flowers.



After some rest they left to meet the mysterious informant who turned
out to be Deia Whitehoney (even though she claimed not to be) and she
told the group that Morrik went to the Herzblatt district to seek the
mysterious red lilies who were known to him due to a man he met some
days ago. He went there with the rest of the young nobles but only
Morrik and Ian Greensprout made it far into the district. They were
surprised by something and got scared, while running Ian hit his head
and lost consciousness. When he woke up, Morrik wasn't there and he
swiftly left. Deia felt responsible for what happened to Morrik since
she was the one to ask him for this flowers and asked the group to bring
closure and resolution to what killed Morrik. She then left.



The group decided to spend the night in the tavern and recover forces
for the upcoming travel to the Herzblatt district.



15 October (Day 5)

When they arrived they met two guards and agreed to say the word
"Banana" when they would like to enter again.



They investigated the Herzblatt district and went into a house, which
walls fell onto Golt and Aeriff. They found out that most of the
buildings had abjuration runes to protect them, but their effect had
worned out with time.



They found old blood stains in one passage and decided to follow it
towards a church and an old crypt. They looked into the church but found
nothing out of the ordinary. The crypt had a poem written on it and they
decided to investigate it further. When they came in, they were greeted
by the ghost of Velen Herzblatt, which established a connection with
Count Virgula and explained that something corrupted his crypt and his
legacy and tasked the group to cleanse the crypt from whatever curse was
cast upon it. He also offered what could be found on his tomb as a
reward for it and offered them to wield his old weapon to fight the evil
within the crypt.



After that the group decided to investigate to seek this weapon and
found a closed door. Jori transformed into a mouse and squished through
an opening to a room with a statue of Selune, a mystical pool with
healing properties and a poem. The rest of the group divided and went to
explore another corridor to find a lever which after activation opened
the door that kept Jori trapped and allowed something from within the
crypt to pass and animate the dead corpses of the Herzblatt family that
resided there. Suddenly the group saw themselves surrounded by a lot of
zombies, skeletons and even a ghast. In a crude fight, Aeriff was
overwhelmed but many undead. Luckily for him, he hold his "arcane focus"
and cast a spell which allowed him to make some of the undead run in
fear of him, he then summoned a spiritual weapon that dealt with as many
undead as he was surrounded by. Next to him where Golt and Virgula who
fought a more powerful undead with an almost unbearable stench and the
ability to paralyze one person with a scratch of its claws. Virgula
suddenly started climbing on the wall while casting spells and Golt
managed to tank the powerful undead while using his magical spear to
defeat what got into his path. They finished the undead rather easy and
then went to help Aeriff with the remaining foes./
/
On the mean time and on the opposite side of the crypt, Jori and Kespien
were also overwhelmed by the amount of zombies and skeletons that raised
from the tombs. Kespien used his ability to enhance his martial
capabilities to hold the undead on the door frame while dealing quite a
bit of damage to them, while Jori went into the pool of mystic water to
have his wounds healed. He then summoned a spirit animal to aid Kespien
and used the magical water to create an ice knife that killed almost all
of the undead remaining.



When all of the enemies were defeated, the two parties met and decided
to use the healing capabilities of the pool to their advantage. They
healed fully the wounds of Aeriff and Kespien and only a little bit of
the ones from Virgula before the pool previously with crystal water,
turned black and the decided to leave it alone. Golt when he saw the
statue of Selune, tried to convince the group to leave him alone in the
room with it, but they refused.



They then tried to investigate the remaining of the crypt but got pretty
scared when they saw a big door from where the dark energy seems to
emanate. They decided to rest on the sacred room of Selune to recover
some of their wounds and they heard someone screaming from far away.
They then decided to abandon the crypt to go seek the weapon that Velen
spoke of in the church, but the main doors were closed. Apparently the
lever that initially activated the doors was deactivated, turning the
lights off and closing the doors. They then played a game of cat and
mouse with something, apparently a flying something, that were playing
with them. Kespien used his magic to detect invisible creatures and so
he managed to find a small Imp that was flying around and seemed to be
the one playing the games. He looked further into his thoughts to see an
obnoxious corpse in front of an altar which was doing some kind of
ritual and offering people to a skull with violet gems in the eyes. The
little imp called this person Master Gloran. Virgula used his "almost
vampiric" powers to suggest that the little imp should help them with
their mission and managing to stop him from activating the lever and
allowing them to go outside.



When they did, they found out that they spent way too much time inside
this crypt and the sun was starting to set down. They then proceed to
investigate the church and they found a registry of the people who
married there, being Velen Herzblatt and Zyralys the first one to do so.
With a name, they decided to go back to the crypt and look for Zyraly's
tomb. They looked into many of the tombs until they finally found it.
This tomb was different than the rest and was beautifully ornamented and
had the figure of an elven woman on top of the tomb. After opening it,
the corpse of Zyralys was intact and she hold a blade on her hands. The
ghost of Velen appeared and shed a tear for her beloved wife. The party
asked for permission to take the blade and Zyralys opened her hands
allowing Kespien to take it. After he did, he felt the power in the
blade almost instantly.



Armed with new courage, they decided to finally go to observe what was
behind that ominous door. Kespien used Espina de Otoño (Velen's Blade)
to break a barrier that was set before the door and they opened it. On
the other side, they saw the same obnoxious figure from the Imps mind
and they saw how he made a hole on the heart of another innocent person
before offering it to the skull. He then turned to the party and said,
so we shall meet again. He was guarded by some zombies and skeletons and
one of them happened to be Deia Sweethoney, who for the aspect of it,
died not so long ago and also had such a hole in her heart. On the back
there was a living woman who seemed to be another sacrifice to the
skull.



Golt made himself big using his innate powers and when he passed through
the frame to start the fright, he stopped for a second and had a vision.
For him it was a lot of time, for the rest just seconds. But when he
came back to his senses, the spear on his hands seemed to have gained
some power and he suddenly covered himself into an ice armor.



The fight began. The adventurers fought well against several undead and
Gloran Herzblatt. Aeriff managed to turn some of the undead present
controlling the situation for a bit. The skull pulsed with dark energy
that damaged and debilitated the combat abilities of the group. Some of
them got new powers and abilities and some others got whispers from
deities or other entities that allowed them to gain use strengths they
didn't know they had yet. They managed to break the barrier that was
protecting the skull and decided to destroy it using Filo de Otoño,
after Aeriff recognized the skull to be a potential Lich phylactery.
When they managed to do so, the little Imp who was invisible all this
time decided to steal one of the gems incrusted in the skull and flew
away towards Velen chamber. Gloran finally fell to the hand of Aeriff
with the magical blade, which turned him into a tree.



Some of the tried to follow the imp or an exit and some helped the
chained woman. They saw as the imp activated a hidden mechanism pulling
a torch from a wall and opening a secret room. Kespien attacked one more
time from distance with magic missiles shaped like daggers and Golt
ordered it to fight him, but the devious Imp was stronger than expected
and survived the darts and ignored the command. He then went into the
room and threw the jewel towards the tomb of Velen, forcing his ghost to
possess his corpse and animating him as a powerful undead.



A fight started in Velens room, which full of soul energy pushed Kespien
to the ground several times and also broke the infrastructure of the
crypt threatening to destroy it. Golt went into the room, attacked Veles
corpse a couple of times and decided that it was time to leave, took an
armor set and started to run. Aeriff managed to save the woman from her
manacles and run away with her for his life. Count Virgula attacked from
a secure distance a couple times and decided that it was time to
retreat. Jori very bravely defended himself from new undead creatures
that surrounded him. Kespien avoided the attacks of Velen's undead body
while attacking it with a magical dagger that he found in the treasure
of the room. The crypt wouldn't hold much longer, only Golt, Jori and
Kespien were still at risk of staying inside. Jori and Golt shouted
Kespien to leave everything and follow them and after some moments of
hesitation, he did.



The group then run for his life avoiding parts of the ceiling that was
falling down and managed to jump just in time into a separate tunnel
that opened at the base of the exit. After taking some breath they
followed the tunnel hoping for an exit and they found it.



The exit connected directly to the hidden garden of the Herzblatt in
which they found the famous red lilies of Dorelta. They also saw the
souls of Morrick Oaktree and Deia Sweethoney that they managed to find
themselves once more before leaving for the afterlife. And also the soul
of Alara, Golts wife, who perished some time ago and which he promised
he will bring her to this exact garden. Some love words were exchanged,
he then poured her ashes into the garden as he promised he would do and
found a ring on it. He then gave his farewell to Alara for the last
time. Before they left the garden, they gathered some of the red lilies
of this sacred garden. They then went back to the Travelers district
where they decided the next steps, had a look at everything they got
from the crypt and took some necessary rest. Among some magical items,
they found a dark book about lichdom apparently stolen from the Temple
of Knowledge and documents addressed to Gloran Herzblatt by a mysterious
figure named the Shadow Auctioneer (signed with a sigil with the
initials T.L.) which seemed to be the facilitator of the book. T.L. was
also the name of a famous criminal from back then when Golt was still a
reputed member of the Radiant Lions, which specialized in stealing
valuable goods. Aeriff also connected the name with Tom Lancil,
apparently someone who managed to get into the Temple of Knowledge and
steal a book about creation of magic weapons.



16 October (Day 6)



When they woke up, Aeriff received a Sending from his master, Sacred
Plume Silica Stein, asking how he was and to prompt him to go to
Lonsaddle soon, so he won't miss the upcoming Arcane Revelry. They then
decided to go meet first the Oaktree family and get the reward they were
promised. They told Mr. Oaktree everything that happened in the crypt,
including showing him the documents of the Shadow Auctioneer. In these
documents there was clearly stated that Lara the honest helped somehow
to smuggle the book into the library of the Mayoress Rulias Clearsong.
This gave Mr Oaktree enough to pull the thread and blame her for
everything that happened. He gave the party their reward, told Kespien
to thank the seeker of the Silvertusk Brotherhood, named Thomas Lawson,
and insisted them to leave his property, for he had more important
matters to handle. He also gave command to Mike to prepare the horses.



Knowing that they might have complicated things for the Mayoress and the
demi humans, the group decided to gain as much time as possible to allow
Jori and Kespien to meet with the Mayoress before the elves did. Jori
used his wild shape to transform into a war horse, which Kespien rode to
the Travellers District. The rest of the party tried to make some time
by using vampiric suggestion towards Mike (which unfortunately wasn't
the one making the travel, but would now need way more time to prepare
for his next travel) and intimidating/casting spells against the two
riders who would go to the other district. The distracting maneuvers
managed to gain some time for Kespien and Jori but not enough to avoid
them reaching the district. Aeriff, Golt and Virgula then decided to
give up and go meet the others on the travelers district.



When Jori and Kespien arrived to the townhall, they met with the
mayoress and explained her what happened. Which made her panic
immediately knowing what would happen next. They read into the documents
of the shadow auctioneer that Lara the honest was involved in everything
and decided to interrogate her. After Kespien used her ability to read
minds, they figured out that Lara was innocent and she only took a
package from a young boy with blond hair, she also mentioned that the
boy smelled a lot like roses. They went into her warehouse to find
nothing anymore. Desperate, they tried to reason with the mayoress and
tried to find a solution to the problem without much success.



When the elves riders arrived, they met alone with the mayoress and
Felosial communicated to the group that Deia Sweethoney, the second
important heir of the noble families had also died. The elves left with
a smile on their face knowing that things will finally change in
Dorelta. Golt, Virgula and Aeriff arrived to the district as the elves
were leaving and joined the rest. They also tried to discuss with the
Mayoress about a way to solve the situation, but unfortunately there was
nothing else that they could do anymore. The Mayoress paid them for
their service and asked them to leave. The party sad for what they had
provoked decided to get some rest and part towards Longsaddle the next
day.

20 October (Day 10)



On the 4th day of travel to Longsaddle via the Prime Road they met a
group of wanderers. A young beautiful human woman named Lapislazuli read
the fortune of the group using different methods. When she read Golt's
fortune, she called him the Sentinel and then collapsed. When she woke
up, she told about this mysterious figure and asked the elder to come
out and tell the story. The Elder was a very old Firbolg woman who told
the story of the Sentinel. She also told that his name wasn't to be
called freely because it was dangerous and that she heard the story by
the majestic bard Abenthy. The group then talked a little bit about the
reasons why there were on the way and figured out that Golt was the
current wearer of the Unheilmeer (the spear he wielded) and that he
needed to bring it to the Ipletherion. Jori suprised to hear that told
him that he also wanted to go to the Ipletherion, because it was the
home of his mother and she needed him. She also asked him to accompany
the wielder of the Unheilmeer there and aid him on his path. Jori also
told that this Iplehterion might be located in Myth Coralis, a place of
wonder where all fish and the live of the sea gather. Aeriff was very
surprised when he heard the name of Myth Coralis, for cities with the
name Myth usually indicated the presence of a Mythal, something he had
been trying to locate. It seemed that destiny brought together some
members of the group, leaving Kespien and Virgula out of this

22 October (Day 12)



At the night of the 6th day of travel to Longsaddle they reached it.
Since it was very late already, Aeriff decided to do a guided tour the
next day and bring them to the best tavern in town, the Broken Scale.
They were surprised to see the tavern packed and the reason was that
there was a show by the famous Helene Soprani, a young halfling
musician. She sung the famous song of "Belmont the liar" which made the
delight of many and the anger of Kespien. Kespien decided that the
tavern wasn't the place for him right now and left it. The other enjoyed
the music until they noticed Kespien absence. They met outside and
discussed how the song made Kespien feel and what he could do against
it. They then decided to enjoy some drinks together and tried several of
the specialities of the Broken Scale. Most of them got intoxicated  due
to the strong drink "The dragon's breath" which Vorskann, the copper
dragonborn innkeeper, created himself. He also told them some of his
best jokes and tested the humor of the group with them.



While enjoying themselves they met some of the patrons:/


- [Eleyna](/docs/players/aeriff/eleyna)

- [Helene
  Soprani](/docs/players/jori/helene-soprani)

The group discovered that Aeriff had a crush on Eleyna and "tried" to

They also heard about several

After many more drinks, some desserts and some discussions they decided
to call it a night. Unfortunately due to the incoming Arcane Revelry,

23 October (Day 13)



After they woke up and some morning excersises by Kespien and Golt to
fight the hangover they decided to explore Longsaddle a little bit. They
made some purchases in the
[Arcanazon](/docs/longsaddle/arcanazon/arcanazon),
they went to the public baths to freshen up, they glanced at the
[Oratorium](/docs/longsaddle/temple-of-knowledge/temple-of-knowledge)
and finally went to the [Temple of
Knowledge's](/docs/longsaddle/temple-of-knowledge/temple-of-knowledge)
main building.



In there they were surprised to meet an elf woman identical to
[Felosial](/docs/dorelta/dorelta-npcs/felosial),
which named seemed to be
[Elosial](/docs/longsaddle/temple-of-knowledge/elosial)
and happened to be her cousin. She took some messages from the group to
call on the Sacred Plume Silica Stein and also professor Ada Gladstone.
Kespien and Jori decided to join the temple for some hours and
investigate some matters. They were aided by small little robotic
creatures named
[Modrons](/docs/longsaddle/temple-of-knowledge/modrons),
which were the core workers of the temple and happened to be the
centerpiece of Aeriff's studies.



The rest met with Sacred Plume Silica Stein and got invited to his house
for dinner that same day, since he was eager to hear all about the
happenings of Aeriff and his new found friends in Dorelta.



They met at the house of Sacred Plume Silica and enjoyed a meal
together. Presentations were done and questions were asked, some of the
topics disucssed were:



- Main schools of the Temple of knowledge and the names of their Sacred
  Plumes
- Discussion about the possibilities to transmute the body back to its
  original form (for Golt and Virgula). Silica will (if he finds the
  time) analyze Golt's case and tell him if he is cursed or what is the
  problem with his physical appareance. Vampirism or any kind of undead
  transformation is non reversible.
