01 November (Day 21)

With everything ready they waited for Jhandril who appeared with a wagon
loaded with many items, some of them the group marked as unnecessary and
forced him to get rid of them.

 

After some discussion they decided to follow the northern road towards
Kor Kuldir and after a while start a travel offroad to the east, towards
the Misty Hills. While on the road, they heard some commotion behind the
next hill and Golt asked Virgula to jump on his horse and scout ahead.
From the top of the hill they saw as a group of potential Radiant Lions
was discussing with a group of dwarven merchants. One of the guards took
a young dwarven woman, mounted her on a horse and run away. They only
fighter looking dwarf tried to confront the guards without much success
and Golt decided to ride swiftly in the direction to inquire what was
going on and potentially solving the situation. Before Golt could
inquire anything, Virgula decided to take action to his hands and cast a
cloud of darkness on the guards, which caused them to withdraw their
weapons and attack Golt to stop him from passing the control. A battle
started and to the surprise of the group, from a nearby hill two bandits
and some kind of sorcerer appeared out of hiding and what seemed to be
to tree trunks, were in fact two half ogres in disguise. The rest of the
group managed to slowly made its way up the hill and saw the conflict
already started.

 

After a dear fight in which the group managed to deal against almost all
of the bandits (one managed to escape and the illusionist managed to
hide invisible and use one of the horses to escape) and the leader,
another bandit and a half ogre surrendered when they saw themselves
outnumbered. Firstly the leader was willing to exchange information with
the group in trade of his liberty but he changed his mind after some
discussions and mostly after one of the other bandits offered to tell
everything he knew for less. Some threats were issued and it ended with
the bandits leader getting unconscious, tied up and left by the dwarves.

 

The bandit explained that their band used to deal with Espuma de Mar but
as of last the supply coming from Puerto Ballena got short and they had
to rely on other means of obtaining money. They assaulted caravans and
kidnapped people with the intent of selling them in the black market. He
also told that their leader was the One eyed man. He got some silver
from Kespien and got told to change his life around and abandoned the
crime life. He said he would go south and start a new life.

 

After that the dwarves asked the Farbound Fellowship to help them and
rescue their daughter from these bandits. The one dwarf guard would
accompany them and they also managed to befriend Spike the Half ogre.
The group agreed to help the dwarves and left in the direction to the
main bandit camp that were told. Night came upon the group and they saw
the bandit camp from the top of another hill. Jori and Virgula decided
to scout ahead and obtain some information about head count and layout
of the camp. Virgula climb the left hill while invisible and Jori
summoned an owl and looked through its eyes. Jori was almost caught
sneaking by a bandit that needed to empty his bowls, but managed to stay
hidden behind a tree.

 

They both managed to gather that there were numerous bandits, half ogre,
the gnome illusionist and the One eyed man who apparently changed his
name to the One eyed Stray and it was a tabaxi with an eye patch, who
almost caught Virgula sneaking. They also noticed a big bag near the
river and the boats in which most probably there were some kidnapped
people ready to be sent down the river.

 

Virgula and Jori made it back safe to the group and conveyed all what
they saw. The group stand before a big decision and started planning
their attack, for they had some kind of surprise, since the bandits knew
of them but not when or how they will attack.

 

The plan was clear, Virgula invisible and Jori as a squirrel will
infiltrate the hideout again using for that the distraction that Golt
riding a horse and Kespien on foot will create in the main gate.
Meanwhile Aeriff, Jhandril, Rolf and Spike will try to infiltrate from
the flank and get ahold of the two bandits on the cliff.

 

Unfortunately for the group, the bandits were informed of them and
attacked right at first sight. They managed to create enough distraction
though that Virgula and Jori infiltrated unnoticed and reached the boat
in which the hostages were taken. Ariff group was also noticed and the
combat started in two flanks.

 

Kespien and Golt managed to take down with some smart tactics most of
the bandits, that is until the gnome wizard and the one eyed stray
appeared. The one eyed stray conjured a panther out of a figurine and
decided to join the fright against Golt and his horse.

 

Aeriff and the rest took down the two guys on the cliff and failed to
convince Spike to help, since the people inside were also his friends.
Rolf almost got killed by some bandits and Jhandril used some of his
magic missiles to take down some other bandits.

 

Virgula jumped invisible into the boat and tripped into the water,
calling the attention of the bandits loading the boat. Jori managed to
jump, still in squirrel form, into the boat unnoticed. Virgula misty
stepped into the boat again and cut the ropes holding the boat to the
shore after it was totally loaded. The last load hit Jori as a squirrel
and turn him back to  his normal form. The boat started to move being
driven by the current and Virgula cast a cloud of darkness on it. The
panther jumped into the boat and gave a bad time to both, managing to
push them over and over into the water.

 

After a hard encounter, the group managed to use new gained abilities
and overcame the bandit forces. Unfortunately for them, the two leaders
managed to escape unnoticed, one invisible and one showing an amazing
speed. The rest of the bandits surrendered and the group managed to save
the hostages and get back to the shore without problems.

 

The group tried to gather as much loot and information as they could.
The bandits appeared to be under the effects of Espuma de Mar, according
to what they have gathered so far. They also found a letter addressed to
a certain person named F.F. written by the One Eyed Stray in a strange
way. They finally decided to leave the bandits leave and go back to the
dwarves.

 

The dwarves were very pleased by the rescue and gave the Farbound
Fellowship a magic amulet that would give them courage in desperate
situations and promised to bring back the hostages to Longsaddle as soon
as possible. They also agreed to give a good word of the name Belmont
and discredit the song if they heard it. They all rest together and
parted ways in the morning.

08 November (Day 28)/
The last three days the red star in the sky was getting bigger and the
weather was getting warmer. At some point during the day they were
contacted from Ralto via the snail Gary. He told them that the time for
the mission they agreed on had arrived and he invited to go into his
bazaar. Seeing that they might get caught in the explosion of the
meteorite that was to impact.

 

They decided to hear Ralto and save their asses. They went into a portal
that opened from the small snail and were surprised when they reached
the bazaar. Many different races of creatures that they didn't know
existed were there. They also learnt that there was some kind of
antimagic field in the bazaar and that it was protected by iron golems.
They went to meet Ralto and had to wait a little bit. They also saw from
the distance some of the other merchants available at the bazaar.

 

Ralto invited them to a private stance inside another big snail and
offered them some beverages. He explained that the meteorite that was
about to impact was made of a metal with one of the highest Yal contents
of all. Apparently Yal is a measurement for the capability of a material
to be enchanted with magic. He wanted the group to gather as much of the
material as possible and bring it to them. In exchange for it, he would
as promised offered them a magic item of his personal stash as their
choosing and allow them to keep coming into his bazaar. They group
agreed and asked about replacing the lost animals and carts, to which
Ralto told them to contact Jhoras. The group then left to meet Jhoras,
who happened to be a talking cat who could provide with any mundane item
(vehicles and living things not included) in a short period of time. He
managed thanks to Ralto's recommendation to provide with a cart and some
mining equipment. Ralto also mentioned that he was trying to free a
possible future merchant that would handle with gems and such. He also
told Kespien that after they completed their mission, he might allow him
to keep some of the metal to reforge an old weapon, but that they will
discuss it when the time comes.

 

The group then decided to go back but time had passed in Norberia.

 

The rest of the day was lost due to time difference between Ralto's

09 November (Day 29)/
Most of the day is lost due to time difference between Ralto's dimension
and Norberia's dimension.

/
The view was shocking. Everything in many miles was burnt out to the
ground and a big crater was to be seen on the distance. While
approaching the crater they were attacked by some kind of elemental
creatures that were born from pieces of metal, most probably from the
meteorite. They managed to deal them without much effort and left to
inspect what was happening in the big crater.

 

Jori decided to summon an owl to scout ahead and he saw a group of very
well equipped dwarves fighting a bigger and more dangerous version of
the elemental creatures who appeared to have been born from half of the
meteorite. The other half of it was intact. The group then was
confronted with a decision to make. Will they help the dwarves or would
they allow them to fight on their own?

 

They decided to approach the battlefield and help the dwarves in their
fight, so they divided in two groups. Kespien would enhance is movement
with magic and would speed up to the other side of the crater, which was
covered on smoke and had limited visibility while the rest would follow.
Jori would go to the right to try and do the same.

 

What they didn't expect is that from the other half of the meteorite
another elemental creature formed ready to attack them. A frenetic fight
started that caught the party flat footed. They started to react as
efficient as possible. Golt focused his attacks on the creature, Kespien
tried to avoid attacks and hit it with magic. Virgula fired some spells
and used the wild magic wand to exchange his clothes for the ones of a
joker and summon two skeletons from the ground. Jori tried the same
strategy as before and created rain on top of the elemental, but this
time unfortunately it was too hot for the amount of water that he could
create and didn't help.

 

The fight brought the party to the limit several times and finally when
almost everything was lost Golt heard a familiar but unexpected voice.
The one of the moon gooddes he despised that much. He saw signals on the
ground and focused his sight on the smoke where he could see a giant
wolf ready to be of help. A present of the goddess, only if he accepeted
it. He hesitated a little bit but finally approach it and therefore he
gained a new mount. But unfortunately something changed in the
Unheilmeer making it less powerful, maybe? Now mounted and with
renovated strenghts went to fight against the elemental once more.

 

The elemental creature managed to summon some lava minions which
happened to be the doom of Kespien. When the big elemental ceased to
exist and exploded in a thousand pieces by the hand of Golt and the
Unheilmeer. The little elementals still alive took the life from Kespien
for good.

 

Unaware to the rest, Kespien saw his family. Once more he was reunited
at home with his mother and father long lost.

 

In the meantime, the dwarves came to see what was going on and help if
necessary. They were shocked to see the two skeletons and asked if there
were any deaths. The group, still in shock were able to answer. The
dwarve cleric, that introduced herself as Nuraguhilda Barrel Braker, a
follower of Moradin and member of the Jade Watchers from Kor Kuldir
offered to help in exchange of the metal from the meteorite. The group
cornered, accepted and so the dwarf started a spell to bring Kespien
back.

 

While Kespien was having a conversation with his parents and enjoying
his time there. His mother told him that he still had things to do and
it wasn't his time to be there. He had to make a choice, stay or leave
and with all the pain in his heart, he decided to live and left the
comfort of his house and family.

 

He woke up extremely weak and full with burns next to the dwarf. The
group quickly went to hug him and check that he was indeed alive. They
shared some tears and comfort words and discuss with the dwarves what
happened.

 

The dwarves were there for the same reason than the party, they were
sent to obtain the high yal metal for Kor Kuldir, since they were
informed by their astrologers of the meteorite they came prepared for
it. The group told the dwarves that they were tasked by a devil to
obtain that metal and the dwarves shortly regreted helping them, but the
group managed to explain things and ask for mercy from the dwarves to
keep at least part of the metal, since they also fought to the end
against the creature.

 

As they were all tired, the dwarves agreed to share camp for the night
and discuss the terms of their previous deal the next day. Kespien asked
the dwarves if some of them would like to join into the cabin and they
politely declined.

 

He created his magic cabin and the group decided to take some time to
assimilate what just happened. Kespien opened his heart and exchange how
he was feeling with the group. He also told his story and how because of
him, his parents died. The group decided to comfort him as they could
and finally it seemed as it somehow worked.

 

The group then decided to discuss the action plan for the upcoming day
with the dwarves and how to try to handle with them in order to get more
ore. After some discussion and as they were very exhausted after the
fights, they all decided to think about it while resting and discuss
again in the morning.

 

When they went to sleep, each of their own thinking about what happened
and or about some nice memories, nightmares decided to hunt their
dreams.

 

It all started with Golt, who was transported to a decaying ship with a
black bird as its figurehead. He met there Lathor, the demon who talked
to him previously and was the emissary of Dagon. He started to make fun
of him, while suddenly dissappeared in terror after an alarming
dangerous presence made itself noticed. Dagon had arrived.

 

Not showing itself, it was certain that this entity was old and powerful
and it could snap Golts hard body with a single thought. Dagon
recriminated Golt's decission and asked him to make ammends or suffer
the consequences. He also showed him what he could become if he didn't
comply, a creature of nightmare named the Sentinel. And warned him that
this entity would be watching and ready to hunt him if needs be. All to
recover the Unheilmeer and complete the mission Dagon entitled Golt to.

 

Golt proud as he ever was tried to outsmart or provoke Dagon, but didn't
manage much. The entity decided to give him an escarment and make him
feel the fear of drowning, when he heard the howling of a wolf and felt
a gentle pull. It was clear that Selune wanted to save him, but he still
didn't want to make a clear statement since he needed time to think
about.

His companions were also having nightmares and it was indeed confirmed

While discussing about what happened, Golt explained the group the

They all discussed about what would be the next steps and if they should

On the mean time Jhandril, with new courage given by the magic item

12 November (Day 32)

They pushed during the day and managed to arrive into the forest at the
base of the Wyvern's Mountains. It started to snow and they noticed that
there were more than usual bones and carcasses of animals, in special
fog bisons. Jori gathered that most of the creatures were hunt for sport
and not for survival as it usually is the way in nature. Whoever hunted
the bisons didn't differentiate between young, old or healthy and most
of the time just ate the guts and the more nutritive parts leaving the
rest to rot. When night came they were starting to get ready to mount
camp inside the magic cabin but were ambushed and surprised by a pack of
winter wolves.

 

Two majestic, enormous and threatening adult winter wolves and four
smaller ones surrounded the group and threatened them to kill them. With
some luck, one of the wolves suggested that the party could be useful to
them and told them to rescue one of their members from the Jotun. The
party without much options, accepted and the Wolves told them to push
the march towards the Jotun cave for the night.

 

13 November (Day 33)

They spent the day travelling with the wolves and arrived to the Jotun
cave at night. The wolves left them there and told them to rescue their
member and that they would be watching them. They better not try to hide
or run since they knew their scent and would pursue them. They group
decided to spent the night risking being caught by the Jotun, but since
almost all their magic resources were depleted, they needed it. Virgula
summoned an illusion in front of the group while Kespien summoned the
cabin which he tried to camouflage it the best it could. They then took
watches and Jori managed to get a view of the Jotun, a double headed
white Ettin which luckily for the group didn't notice them. The group
managed to finish a long rest unnoticed.

 

14 November (Day 34) Joris Birthday

 

When they woke up they started doing preparations for infiltrating in
the Jotun's cave. While doing so, Jori received several mental messages
from his beloved ones, since it was his birthday. It seemed to be the
last time for that to come, but adventurer's don't get to choose.
Virgula got a bit too excited and shouted loud enough for the Jotun to
come out, but luckily Jori pulled out a spell scroll of fog cloud and
managed to hide the group, letting them remain unnoticed. They then
decided on a plan to scout first and Virgula invisible while climbing
the icy walls of the cave when inside. He saw the Jotun eating something
in a corner and a winter wolf's cub inside a cage on the opposite side
of the cave. He managed to come back very stealthily and unnoticed.

 

He retaliated what he saw and the group decided that the best course of
action would be to give the wolve a potion of diminution that Golt's had
and make him slide through the cage. When about a minute had passed
since Virgula go in, they would create a distraction outside the cave to
call the Jotuns attention and make things easier for Virgula. The plan
was done, they all prepared and Jori even called for nature to help them
be sneaky and hide better. After that Virgula went into the cave again
unnoticed and managed to come very close to the wolf. Golt created an
illusion of a Wyvern's scream and the Jotun took his weapon, summoned an
icy spear in his free hand and left the cave shouting in an unknown
language.

 

When the Jotun got out of view for Virgula and while he was getting
ready, still invisible, to give the wolve the potion. The wolve changed
shapes and turn that of a half orc, wearing wooly fabric and wielding a
big staff. He cursed the situation, transformed himself into a rat and
started to run away from the cave.

 

While the Jotun was making its way to the cave's entrance, Virgula and
the rat went out of the cave. The rest of the party managed to stay
hidden within the fog cloud with the help of the blessings of Jori. Some
clever uses of minor illusions manage to confuse the Jotun enough to
make him bored and leave. The party left the cave and decided while
Kespien was trying to prepare his Hut for protection. The rat came back
and turned himself into Thrag the Half-orc druid. He was some kind of
protector of the misty hills and specially the fog bison's that lived
there. He despised the winter wolves but didn't want to kill them
directly. Therefore he kidnapped one of the wolves cubs and make them
believe that the Jotun got it, by passing as the cub itself. His plan
was to make the Jotun and the wolves fight with the hope of restoring
the power balance in nature. Unfortunately the party managed to ruin his
plans and they agreed to help him. He also offered to charm the winter
wolves cub to give the party the breath they were seeking if they helped
him with the wolves problem. He gave them a horn that they could use to
call him and wished them luck.

 

The party then went into the cabin and started making plans. Jori also
brew some dried kelp tea and used some spices to enrich it and also to
celebrate his birthday.

 

When the party went out of the hut, they were already surrounded by the
wolves. Without trying to mediate with them, Kespien started the fight
by firing a firebolt at them.

 

During the fight Golt summoned his mount and managed to frightened some
of the wolves while defending the group from some of the wolves attacks.
Jori summoned some spirits that took the form of giant goats to help
defend them from the wolves. Kespien tried to use his new lightning
abilities with no success but managed to get the urn ready to accumulate
some of the desired breath of the wolves. And Virgula managed to land a
couple of very well placed fireballs taking the live of some of the
wolves. Jhandril was attacking the wolves from a healthy distance.

 

The group managed to kill all the wolves with some clever maneuver. Jori
made the plants around be thick and overgrown limiting the movement of
the wolves and their escape. Kespien managed to hit a couple of times
the wolves and Virgula muted due to some random wild magic who caused
him to exhale purple bubbles out of his mouth every time he wanted to
speak throw daggers at the wolves. Golt from the top of his wolf and
with an enlarged version of the Unheilmeer took good account of the last
wolf. When all of the wolves were killed, the group, exhausted but alive
took some fangs of the wolves and loaded one carcass on Golt's wolf and
Jori's giant goats. They decided to leave the area to avoid unnecessary
confrontations with the Jotun.

 

When they felt they were secure, they used the horn that Thrag gave them
and not much later a huge hoard of fog bisons appeared. One of them, the
biggest one, was Thrag in his wild shape form. He turn back into his
half-orc form and congratulate the group for his task. He also invited
them to spend the night at his hut where he will provide for nourishment
and winter clothing for the group. Jori also noticed that the herd
seemed to be somehow charmed, since they weren't behaving similar to the
first one they found.

 

They all rode the fog bison's (besides Golt who rode along on his wolf)
towards the hut. Thrag convinced them to abandon the winter wolves
carcass and to leave it for the Jotun to take care. He openly despised
the winter wolves. A fact that was confirmed when the group entered his
hut. On the way Jori noticed that the grass on the area was almost
inexistant and was for sure not enough for the amount of fog bisons that
lived there.

 

Once in the hut, they saw that it was decorated with skulls of
carnivores. It was a simply hut but equipped with enough amenities to
comfortably accommodate a humanoid. Thrag provided some food and drink
and told them that the next day he will bring the cub and convince it to
provide with the desired winter wolves breath. Before laying to rest,
Jori heard some knocking outside and decided to go investigate. In a
tree's trunk, a doorknob and the silhouette of a door appeared,
reminding him to the first day he met his spiritual totems some days
ago.

 

He opened the door and the animals appeared in their spectral forms to
wish him happy birthday and to tell him that he was not alone, that they
were always there with him to protect and aid him.

When they reached the mountains they decided to climb it. Golt took care

19 November (Day 38)

 

They kept ascending with similar strategies and everything was fine
until the evening of the day. They were travelling through a narrow pass
between two mountains when they saw the shadow of a big flying creature.
They quickly hid with the help of Jori's magic and managed to stay
mostly hidden. A big wyvern, but small in comparison with the white one,
covered with scars and wounds and with a big chunk of its tail missing
perched on top of the mountains trying to make sure that what it thought
it saw, was still there. And unfortunately, before the sun went behind

several feet away of the group, to a nearby elevation. The small wyvern
fought for his life with little success, and the white one with all its

elevation. The group stood hidden trying to ascertain in which direction
the white wyvern left and deciding if they will try their luck and find
another smaller wyvern or they will really have to fight against the big

While preparing for sleep, Jhandril asked Virgula if they could have a

Virgula told Jhandril that he didn't really kept many secrets and maybe

20 November (Day 39)

 

 When they woke up they decided to follow the path that the white wyvern

They climbed the last part and after crossing the clouds reached the

in special a very useful water walk that allowed the party to walk
unhindered in the snow. Golt decided to approach the Wyvern's nest and
found out a small wyvern, an unhatched egg and the remains of a dead
humanoid. The party took positions according to the plan and Golt didn't
hesitated and killed the small wyvern. Its dying growl alerted the
parent and it crawled from the opposite side of the mountain.

 

The fight started, the Wyvern a majestic creature had some kind of
innate magic and covered himself on an icy armor, making it stronger and
more resistant to damage. The party unleashed all their might on the
wyvern.

 

Virgula cast several fireballs from a safe distance and used the unicorn
wand to create duplicates of himself and get a strange urge to drink
alcohol. He also manifested a shadowy hound with the appearance of a
chihuahua, named [Count
Donut.](/docs/players/count-virgula/count-donut)

Jori managed to restrain the Wyvern to the ground avoiding its flying

Kespien and Golt tanked the attacks of the enraged wyvern and hit it

The wyvern tried to get rid of the party, using tornado strong winds

Finally they put the wyvern in a very dangerous position and all in a

The several fireballs, together with the dying roar of the wyvern and

After a short breather, they decided to cast the Hut and inspect the

They found:

A +1 leather armor

A wyverns egg

Some pages from a stolen tome of the Temple of knowledge

A note from someone named Tom Lancil and addressed to Dondarrion Fairbug

2 spell scrolls (skywrite and Enhance ability)

2 healing potions

One agate very polished

The party met with Jhandril who brought them to Halphanis house.

 

They informed him about the journey and the dangers they faced.

about the Unheilmeer and the effects on Golt's psyche. He explained why
he asked Jhandril to lie to the group and that he was trying the party

tried and failed, was way more dangerous than the one they just partook
and therefore he needed to be sure that he wouldn't be sending some
innocent and unprepared people to a certain death.

 

Since the group proved to be skilled and worthy enough, he rewarded them

predecessor Frederic Belmont, had. He never told him where he got it
from and also why the route marked in the map was so odd.

 

He also told the group, if they wondered why he asked for such estrange
items and the group was surprised to learn that he wanted to "kind of"
bribe the other Sacred Plumes to allow one of the party members to
participate in the upcoming exam to join the Temple of Knowledge. He
gladly offered this chance to one of the party members, which after

died during childbirth some years ago and she left behind an albino son.

Marsandians, being Elipsis one of them.

 

The son was living in an orphanage where he was bullied by the other
kids and even the caretakers. He decided to adopt him, mostly because of
the memories he had of his master but mainly because he felt lost in the

He told Jhandril, that his real name was Dash Virgula and that he was
indeed, the son of Elipsis Virgula and therefore Count virgulas nephew.

 

He shortly removed the magic that masked his heritage and Jhandril,

When Virgula wanted to run behind him, Halphanis stopped him and told

Virgula was moved by the experiences of his brother and further

After he came back to ground, he met his wife and somehow the Unheilmeer

Halphanis did some magic on the lance and managed to locate three

He told Halphanis the name Lathor and Dagon. Also mentioned the Sentinel
