---
title: "Healing potions"
sidebar_position: 1
---

Drinking a healing potion outside of combat always heals the maximum
amount.

 

Drinking a healing potion within combat can be done in two ways:

 

- As an action --/> It will heal you fully
- As a bonus action --/> You need to roll for the specific potions dice

 

Any other non healing potion takes an action to drink or apply.
