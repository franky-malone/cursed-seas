---
title: "Lingering injuries"
sidebar_position: 7
---

If a critical hit brought you to 0 hit points or less, you have to roll
a saving throw according to the damage taken of 5+ damage taken. If you
fail, you might suffer a lingering injurie that can only be healed by
high level magic (like regenerate, greater restoration, or such).

 

Examples:

 

If you took bludgeoning damage, roll for Constitution

 

If you psychic damage, roll for Intelligence
