title: "Crafting healing potions"
sidebar_position: 12
---

- Making a healing potion is a Downtime task. In order to infuse healing
  potions you need the following:/
   

  - suitable place (apothecary, laboratory or alchemist's workshop),
  - special ingredients priced at 25 GP per potion,
  - herbalist kit,
  - have access to the “cure wounds” spell,

   

  If you have all of those things, you can try infusing a potion or
  potions. This requires a period of 8 hours per potion or batch of
  potions. This period can be done during what would be a long rest, but
  you would suffer a level of exhaustion at the end of the process due
  to lack of sleep./
  /
  If you infuse only one potion, assuming that the aforementioned
  materials are present, you will automatically be successful. If you
  want to infuse a batch of potions, a proficiency test will be
  performed with the herbalist kit (Wisdom + proficiency bonus if you
  are proficient with the kit) according to the following table:/
  /
  2 potions --/> DC 10/
  3 potions --/> DC 12/
  4 potions --/> DC 15/
  5 potions --/> DC 18/
  A failure of 5 or less means consuming 25% more resources and time to
  make the batch./
  A failure of 6 or more results in a 50% increase in the cost and time
  to finish the batch and a 50% chance of spoiling 1 potion in the
  batch./
  /
  To make better potions, the time and cost will increase according to
  the following table:/
   

  |                           |                   |                 |             |
  |---------------------------|-------------------|-----------------|-------------|
  | Potion type               | Spell required    | Cost per potion | Time needed |
  | Greater Healing (4d4+4)   | Cure Wounds lvl 3 | 100 GP          | 3 days      |
  | Superior Healing (8d4+8)  | Cure Wounds lvl 5 | 700 GP          | 1 week      |
  | Supreme Healing (10d4+20) | Cure Wounds lvl 7 | 5,000 GP        | 3 weeks     |
