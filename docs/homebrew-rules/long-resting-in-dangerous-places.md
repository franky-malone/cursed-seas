---
title: "Long resting in dangerous places"
sidebar_position: 4
---

(Open to discussion)



You would only recover all your hit points after a long rest if you were
able to sleep in a secure place (e.g. a city, an inn, a watched
environment). When in a dungeon or outside, the DM will determine if you
are in a secure place.



Otherwise you would only recover half your hit dice, your spell slots
and abilities that recharge after a long rest.



Short rests are not affected and they work as usual.
