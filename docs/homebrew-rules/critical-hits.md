A critical hit will always do maximium damage on the original die and
the second die will be rolled as usual. This will apply to player
characters and enemies as well. All other damage dice are treated as
normal (e.g. Sneak attack, Smite, etc...)



Example:



If you score a critical hit with a one handed longsword you will deal 8
damage + 1d8 extra for the critical hit.
