## Overview

**Amirah Sephira Al-Marish** is the daughter of the majestic Djinn King **Husam al-Bali ben Nafhat al-Yugayyim**.

A powerful and prestigious figure among the Djinn, Amirah bears numerous titles associated with the winds and skies.

## Titles

Amirah Sephira Al-Marish is known by many titles:

- **Magister of the Clouds and Daughter of the Breeze**
- **Rider of the Four Winds**
- **Princess of the Birds**
- **Defender of the Skies**
- **Queen of all the Djinn of the Winds of Levant**

## Tournament of Valor

Amirah is believed to be the mysterious figure responsible for organizing the **Tournament of Valor**, held in [Nersand](/regions/nersand/) once every **100 years**.
