# Urs Diaries

## Entry I

> Ralto and I struck true fortune. The ore veins in the Ethereal border are extremely rich in **Yal**!
>
> He swears he knows where the **Tome of Elemental Enchantment** could be. He talks of a Faun from the Feywild that hoards books.
>
> I trust his instinct, though that usually means danger.

---

## Entry II

> The creature follows us still.
>
> Its eyes burn like molten silver; its breath freezes sound itself. We thought it a guardian, but it stalks like hunger given shape.
>
> Ralto insists we can outrun it through a planar fold.
>
> I pray his math holds true.

---

## Entry III

> Something went wrong.
>
> I felt the Weave's instability.
>
> I woke in a forest that weeps blood, beneath trees with faces that watch, without knowing how I landed here or how much time has passed.
>
> I am alone. Ralto is nowhere to be seen.
>
> The air itself hums with hunger, and I have blanks in my memory.
>
> The light won’t stay lit much longer.

---

## Entry IV

> Between the changes, I’ve managed to build a home from shadowwood and a workshop in a nearby cave.
>
> The ore from this land hums with pain. But I can't temper it. Nobody can.
>
> Perhaps I can build something to control it.
>
> The shadows are too long, and even though I have found no other civilized people, I can't shake the thought that I'm constantly being watched.

---

## Entry V

> This place… it breathes through me.
>
> Something lives by the water.
>
> A distant weeping that remembers being flesh.
>
> It seeps into the soul.

---

## Entry VI

> When I hammer, I hear her cry.
>
> When I sleep, I see a crown.
>
> I must resist her call.
>
> I am losing the battle.
>
> **I must stay me.**
