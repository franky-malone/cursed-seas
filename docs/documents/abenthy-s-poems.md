Plaque /#1 – The Poet’s Journey

Through Urbald’s heartwood, where silver leaves swayed,

Past coral towers where moonlight never fades,

Over clouds enslaved by a giant’s cruel whim,

To fire’s cradle, where the world was first born —

Thus was the Moonweaver’s given form.

 

Plaque /#2 – The Lute’s Craft

Wood that remembers whispers of the fey,

Coral kissed by the tides of the mythal bay,

Pegs forged from iron above the storm’s crown,

Fire-opal tears from the mountain’s own frown.

 

Plaque /#3 – The Poet’s Deception

The bard sang not the truth, but what was asked.

A coward’s mask for a hero’s task.

What is buried here is not all that was done —

Seek not the missing verse, lest it be sung.

 

Plaque /#4 – The Hidden Verse

Stones and graves hide nothing here,

Tombs are empty, cold and clear.

Around the lights where shadows fall,

Golden curtains line the hall.

Every night a silent show,

Fake applause and soft light glow.

Laughter once filled up this place,

On wooden boards before each face.

Overhead the rafters soar —

Real treasure waits beneath the floor.
