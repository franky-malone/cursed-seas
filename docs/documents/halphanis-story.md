title: "Halphanis Story"

---

I arrived in [Longsaddle](/longsaddle) many years ago with little more than a dream and no money to my name.

From a young age, history fascinated me the stories of ancient events, the echoes of past civilizations. With the meager sum I had saved, I set out to begin my studies at the [**Temple of Knowledge**](/factions/temple-of-knowledge/). Through sheer effort and determination, I earned a scholarship, awarded by none other than the former Sacred Plume of Archaeomancy, [**Master Ellipsis Virgula**](/factions/temple-of-knowledge/leaders/ellipsis-virgula).

Master Virgula was remarkable.

From the first moment I met him, I sensed he was different from the other scholars at the temple. He was strict, yet kind a tutor who became a friend, and eventually, a father figure to me.

He was a rather peculiar individual, especially due to his fondness for beets, which he ate almost every day. Yet he was remarkable in so many ways. His talent for restoring old artifacts and deciphering ancient texts was unparalleled. His fluency in virtually every ancient language and dialect was astounding.

Despite my own accomplishments as a scholar, **I still haven't matched his contributions to the temple.**

He was the foremost authority on Marsandian history. His books on the war are still regarded as essential reading for young archaeomancers.

It wasn't until much later that I understood the reason behind his expertise.

Master Virgula often spoke of his brother, **Ponto**, who had raised him after their parents died. Ponto had somehow been involved in the war and had tragically lost his life a wound that never quite healed for my mentor.

---

One day, while researching old manuscripts, Master Virgula stumbled upon a Selûnite legend about a group known as the **Crescent Wave**.

They were champions of the Moonmaiden who defended **Myth Coralis**, a mythical city supposedly located in a long-lost archipelago called **Fabros**, from a great evil using the power of a Mythal.

For reasons I couldn't understand at the time, this tale consumed him.

He became obsessed with uncovering the location of Fabros and Myth Coralis, using every resource available to a Sacred Plume and, I suspect, some beyond those.

Around that same time, a catchy song about the sailor [**Manfred Belmont**](/factions/belmont-family/manfred-belmont) became popular.

The song told the story of a sailor who claimed to have reached some [*Cursed Islands*](/regions/cursed-islands) and returned with riches that no one had ever seen.

Master Virgula, being a historian, believed that every song concealed some fragment of truth. He suspected that these Cursed Islands might be the legendary Fabros.

He began collecting every version of the song he could find, hoping to unravel the truth behind it.

*Why he believed this, and why those islands consumed him so completely, remained a mystery to me.*

Not long afterward, **Master Virgula left Longsaddle without telling anyone.**

A year passed without word from him. In my desperate loneliness, I secretly entered his private sanctum.

There, among his notes, I finally discovered the reason for his obsession.

He had been exchanging letters with a Marsandian woman living in disguise in [Gardis](/regions/gardis/). In those letters, he described his discovery of Mythals and his belief that one could be found in Myth Coralis.

He intended to harness the power of such an artifact to fuel a spell capable of **turning back time to save his brother, stop Queen Sanderia, and prevent the war.**

The letters also confirmed something I had long suspected but never dared to ask.

**Master Virgula was himself a Marsandian, a survivor of the catastrophe.**

His final letter, written before his departure, spoke of his imminent journey to Fabros and how much he had enjoyed the last days they had spent together.

His disappearance weighed heavily on my heart, especially after learning what he intended to accomplish.

*Was he a fool for believing that a mere mortal could harness such power? Or would he become a legend the first to achieve the impossible?*

I needed to find him, speak to him, and decide whether I would help him or stop him.

And so, determined to find my mentor, I set out in search of him or of Fabros, where I believed he had gone.

---

My only lead was the same one Master Virgula had followed: the Belmont song about the [Cursed Islands](/regions/cursed-islands).

I followed rumors and whispers until I met the man who would become my captain and best friend, [**Frederick Belmont**](/factions/belmont-family/frederick-belmont), a direct descendant of the Manfred celebrated in the song.

Frederick was a man whose sense of destiny ran as deep as the ocean itself.

He captained the Black Albatros**, a ship that had suffered its fair share of accidents and could barely stay afloat.

After sharing a few drinks, he revealed that he possessed an old map of the Cursed Islands, apparently a family relic.

Days later, I managed to convince him to let me examine it.

The map was faded, dirty, and almost unreadable to an inexperienced eye. Fortunately, I had learned a thing or two from Master Virgula about restoring old artifacts.

While I worked on the map, Frederick and I grew close.

He had his own reasons for wanting to reach the Cursed Islands. He often spoke of destiny and of clearing his family name, which had been tarnished by bards and jokers.

I quickly lost count of how many fights we got into because someone made a joke about his name.

Once I had finished restoring the map, on a day when we may or may not have been drunk, **we agreed to gather a crew and sail for the islands.**

Finding the right people took some time, partly because of Frederick's reputation, but eventually we assembled a good crew.

Frederick then asked me to finalize the preparations for the voyage. He had to leave for a while, claiming that family matters required his attention back in [Gardis](/regions/gardis/).

During his absence, I assumed command of the ship and worked closely with our navigator, [**Irina**](/other-npcs/irina) the love of my life.

From the very first moment we exchanged words, I knew we were made for each other.

Fortunately for me, she felt the same.

Not long afterward, we were married.

---

A couple of months later, Frederick returned.

**But he was no longer the man we had known.**

The cheerful captain had grown quiet and contemplative. He became distant, spending hours alone in his cabin or staring toward the horizon, as though searching for something only he could see.

Some claimed to have heard him speaking to himself in his chambers, repeating a single word over and over.

*Ipletherion.*

He had also brought back a weapon unlike anything I had ever seen: a spear he called [**Unheilmeer**](/important-items/unheilmeer).

It was forged from a metal as dark as the deepest waters. Frederick never allowed anyone close enough to examine it, but I could feel a strange presence emanating from it.

At last, we set sail for the [Cursed Islands](/regions/cursed-islands).

I hoped to find Fabros, Myth Coralis, and, most importantly, my mentor.

We sailed for a long time. When we believed we were finally drawing close to our destination, tragedy struck.

*The sea is unforgiving to naive sailors.*

A brutal storm engulfed us and shattered our ship.

**The Black Albatros was lost to the waves, along with my hopes of happiness and my spirit.**

To this day, I remain the only known survivor.

I washed ashore with nothing but grief and regret.

*Only the gods know why they spared me that day. I wish they had spared Irina instead.*

---

I gathered what little I had left and returned to [Longsaddle](/longsaddle).

I tried scrying, divination, and every other means at my disposal to search for Irina, Frederick, and the rest of the crew.

**All my efforts were futile.**

Time passed, and I fell into a deep depression.

Until I adopted [**Jhandril**](/factions/temple-of-knowledge/members/jhandril-dash-virgula) and took him as my apprentice.

*He helped me overcome my loss.*
