Former fiancee of Morrick Oaktree.

 

She risked being punished and escaped his house to meet with the group
and tell them what she knew about Morrik's death. She got captured by
Telrond Whiterose after she informed the adventurers and her soul was
taken from her using the phylactery of Gloran. Her soul reunited with
Morrik's and they both parted to the afterlife together.
