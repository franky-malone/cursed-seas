Half-elf woman. Mayoress of Dorelta. She is a calm woman but the concern
of the current situation seems to have her on edge. She was looking for
someone to help find out the culprit of the recent murder of the eldest
son of the Oaktree family and avoid like that a political conflict with
the elf nobles.



She got in trouble with the local nobles after the party gave
information to Mr. Oaktree that someone stored a dangerous book on her
library. It is uncertain what will happen to her now.
