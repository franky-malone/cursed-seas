Founders of Dorelta. They moved from Everlor in the Misty Forests into
what is today known as the Autumn Forest. After many years, they allowed
humans to settle in, what caused them several altercates with the other
noble houses of Dorelta. They had a district only for themselves, that
was continuously abandoned until it was empty. At some point something
corrupted that district and it has been since unoccupied.
