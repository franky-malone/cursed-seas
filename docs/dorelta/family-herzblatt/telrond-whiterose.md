![](telrond-whiterose-assets/telrond-whiterose_files/image001.png)

It was found in the Traveller's Inn of Dorelta. He didn't say much,
because he left inmediately after being prompted by Count Virgula about
his life status and noticing that Golt sensed something on him. Research
afterwards show that he was here looking for red lillies that blossomed
only once a year in Dorelta. He wore a very strong roses perfume which
apparently was used to mask a rancid smell.

It happened to be Master Gloran Herzblatt in disguise.
