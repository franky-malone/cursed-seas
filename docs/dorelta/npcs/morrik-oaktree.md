---
title: "Morrik Oaktree"
sidebar_position: 15
---


Eldest son of the Oaktree family. Young elf male.



He was found dead next to the watermill of the noble district. His heart
had been taken out leaving behind a single hole. The rest of the body
presented no further signs of violence.



His soul reunited with Deia's and they both parted to the afterlife
together.
