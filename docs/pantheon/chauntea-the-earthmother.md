Goddess of life, bounty, and agriculture, who viewed herself as the
embodiment of all things agrarian. She had two aspects, one agricultural
that was almost exclusively prayed to in rural areas and one as a mother
goddess, in whose aspect she collected faith from everybody who felt
secure at home
