God of war. His dogma was primarily concerned with honorable battle,
forbidding cowardice and encouraging the use of force of arms to settle
disputes. His large worshiper-base had a lot of soldiers in it. His
church's cultural achievement was to make a set of rules for war
acceptable among various nations.
